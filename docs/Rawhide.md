# Rawhide — What's New (Nightlies)

These notes feed the in-app "Update Available" dialog for Rawhide / cutting-edge builds.
**Only list what landed after the last shipped nightly.** Clear this section when a new nightly goes out — delete the old bullets; do not accumulate them.

Last shipped nightly: `rawhide.20261001.06827b2`. Everything below is unreleased.

## Recent improvements (unreleased — ships in the next build)

- 📚 **Porch Stories Studio** — a second story engine beside the original (now called **Quick**). Studio interviews your cast, double-checks every planning step, writes beat by beat with a continuity check, and patches only the lines that slipped. Each story opens as a studio: Structure, Write, Read, a **Director** that turns a plain-words note into edits you can tick, apply and undo, plus Cast, Relationships, Lore & continuity and a run log. Porch Stories got a whole new look to go with it — a warm, book-shop studio with a shelf of your stories, a four-step wizard (Idea, Cast, Shape, Engine) and the same layout on desktop and phone. Any job can run on any model you have set up — the chat model, your worker, or a KoboldCpp / oMLX / LM Studio / cloud host of your choice, with local models swapping onto the GPU the way chat already does. On models that support it, the planning and review steps use real tool calls (plain tags are the fallback), and **Stop** ends any run safely. Chat-based stories still use the chat as canon and old stories open unchanged.
- 🌱 **Stories from a chat now use its Journal and Growth Rings** — when a chat is distilled into a story timeline, that chat's Journal memories are placed at the moments they happened and must appear, and its Growth Rings show how the character changed, including growth that later faded. Pinned memories are always kept. The timeline card shows it all, on desktop and phone. Only that chat's memories are used, and stories already distilled stay as they are until you Redistill.
- 🧭 **Rewrite arc** — a new button on a Studio story's bible card rewrites the inciting incident, themes, twists and threads together, and leaves the world, cast and interviews alone. Same on the phone.
- 🩹 **A story bible that stopped part-way now finishes itself** — if the bible stopped before its arc was written, Autopilot and Build acts write the arc first instead of planning acts without an inciting incident or themes.
- 🪟 **Porch Stories and Waifu Coder take the whole window** — the app sidebar now shows only on the main Chats screen, and the tab strip above Porch Stories wears the studio's colours. The Chats tab brings the sidebar back.
- 🔎 **Start from a chat has a search box** — type a character or chat name to find it, even in a library of thousands. Each chat shows how many messages it has, very short ones are marked, and chats where you never wrote anything are left out. Same on the phone, where the list now loads in one go.
- 🎭 **A faithful retelling takes its genre and mood from the chat** — the Genre and Mood pickers are hidden for a faithful retelling, so a pick can no longer pull the story away from how the chat played. Choose "Inspired by" to set your own. Same on the phone.
- 📏 **A faithful retelling fits its length to the chat** — a small chat now starts on the shortest length, and if you pick one the chat cannot fill, the Shape step says so in plain words. Faithful stories also no longer bring in new named characters while planning scenes. Same on the phone.
- 🖼️ **Expression packs start from the character's current picture** — if you replace a character's portrait, a new expression pack is built from the new picture instead of an older expression. Existing expressions are used only when the character has no portrait. Same on the phone.
- 🧩 **ComfyUI graphs with boxed-up nodes keep your settings** — when a saved graph exposes a setting on the outside of a subgraph and that setting feeds more than one node inside, every one of those nodes now gets your value. Before, only one did. Same on the phone.
- ⏱️ **Stories with a slow Review model write faster, and say where the time went** — when a check finds a continuity slip, the Prose model now makes the patch; Review only judges. The run log has a new "Time by job" summary with the model each job used, and says in plain words when checks are what is slowing a story down. The Engine step explains that Review runs after every beat. Same on the phone.
- ↔️ **My messages on the right** — a new switch at the top of Settings → General puts your messages back on the right, with your picture on the right, the way chat looked before. Off by default, so your messages stay on the left like the character's. On the phone and in the browser your messages now sit on the left too, to match the app, and the same switch moves them back.
- 🔁 **Story jobs on a local model stop reloading it every call** — a Porch Stories job that uses its own local KoboldCpp model now keeps that model loaded for the whole run. Before, it was unloaded and loaded again for every single step. And if something else reloads the engine in the middle of a run (a restart, loading a model in Settings, a chat reply), the job puts its own model back for its next step instead of finishing the run on the chat model.
- 🛑 **Stopping the app's KoboldCpp leaves yours alone** — on Mac and Linux, stopping or cleaning up the app's engine no longer shuts down a KoboldCpp you started yourself from somewhere else. That holds even when yours loads a model kept in the app's engine folder.
- 🧾 **Generated KoboldCpp presets are written correctly** — a preset now names the graphics card you chose in Settings (before, the choice was written in a form KoboldCpp ignored and every NVIDIA card was used), carries the chat template setting and the model's vision file so both survive a live model swap, and offers all five cache compression levels (f16, bf16, 8-bit, 5-bit, 4-bit). A preset made in KoboldCpp's own launcher is read with every setting kept.
- 🧩 **Your own presets start exactly as you wrote them** — a preset you made, in the app or in KoboldCpp's own launcher, reaches KoboldCpp with every setting intact: several graphics cards, CUDA options, MoE layer counts, cache sizes, and anything you left for KoboldCpp to decide. The app changes only the model, the chat template and the vision file, and switches sliding window off when the preset has it on together with fast forward (that pairing degrades output; the engine log says when it happens). A preset can name its model by a path relative to the engine folder. A preset that can't be read is refused in plain words, and Start keeps working afterwards.
- 🔒 **A preset that would run programs or open KoboldCpp to the internet is refused** — some settings in a KoboldCpp preset make it download and start programs, open a public tunnel to your computer, lend your graphics card to strangers or serve files to anyone who asks. A preset with any of them switched on is not started, and Start, a live model change, a helper or story model, the preset editor's MMQ timing and picking it on the phone all say so in plain words and name the settings, so you can remove them or pick another preset. Presets saved by KoboldCpp itself have them switched off and start as before, and everything else in your preset still starts exactly as you wrote it.
- 🧠 **KoboldCpp now fits your model to your graphics card itself** — "GPU Layers" is **Automatic** for everyone: KoboldCpp works out how much of the model fits on the card and puts the rest in system memory, which also handles MoE models properly. A "Set layers myself" switch brings the number back if you want it. The Auto-Configure button is gone, since its estimate was the thing getting this wrong. A machine where Settings was never opened now uses its graphics card instead of the CPU, and so does one where some backend switches were turned off and the rest never chosen (Settings already said "automatic" there). The memory lock is off unless you turn it on. Same control in Settings, Model Settings and the character creator; the web Hardware panel shows which mode you're on.
- ⚡ **AMD cards on ROCm get flash attention** — ROCm now follows the Flash Attention setting like other cards: on a 16 GB AMD card it made replies up to 2.6 times faster and fit more of the model on the card. If KoboldCpp ever stops on the first reply with it on, the app starts it again without it and remembers that for your computer.
- 🛡️ **Gemma 4 no longer crashes on Vulkan** — on AMD and Intel cards using Vulkan, KoboldCpp stopped on Gemma 4's first reply with flash attention on. The app now turns it off for that model there (in its own launches and in generated presets), and says why in the engine log. The chat cache is full size in that case, since compressing it needs flash attention.
- 💬 **When KoboldCpp stops, the app says why** — running out of graphics memory (and what to try: a smaller context, a smaller batch, stronger cache compression), stopping in the middle of a reply, or a model file it cannot read, in plain words instead of an exit code.
- 🧓 **An outdated KoboldCpp says so** — KoboldCpp older than 1.112 cannot read the settings the app gives it; instead of starting it to fail, the app now says to update it.
- 🔒 **The app's KoboldCpp now answers only this computer** — it used to listen on every network your computer is on, so another device on the same Wi-Fi could read your latest reply or make it drop the model mid-chat. Now only this computer can talk to it, and a preset's own "host" setting no longer changes that. Your phone and browser still reach the app through the Web Server (Settings → Advanced), which has its own login.
- 🛑 **Stop works while KoboldCpp is still getting ready** — pressing Stop right after Start, while the app is still checking your computer and the model file, now cancels the start. Before, the press did nothing, KoboldCpp started anyway, and a second Stop was needed. Quitting the app at that moment cancels the start too. Same on the phone, where Stop can now be pressed while KoboldCpp is starting.
- 🚫 **Start says so when KoboldCpp can't run at all** — if the KoboldCpp program was deleted, blocked or isn't allowed to run, Start now says that in plain words instead of doing nothing.
- 🛡️ **A damaged model file can no longer freeze the app** — picking a model or attaching a photo reads the model file to check what it holds. A damaged or deliberately broken file could make that read go round forever; it now stops at the bad part.
- 🐧 **New presets on Linux get the right thread count** — a processor running two threads per core was counted twice, so a new preset asked for twice as many CPU threads as there are cores.
- ✍️ **Presets can guess ahead** — the preset editor now sets how many tokens a draft model guesses at each step, and offers the model's own built-in draft heads when the model file has them. A preset's plain words say what guesses ahead, and that guessing ahead and a vision file should not be used together.
- 🧠 **A helper model's thinking follows its own template** — the "stop thinking" limit sent with Realism checks and story steps now goes by the model actually loaded. A helper or story model that always thinks no longer thinks at length during checks, and one that listens no longer gets a limit meant for the chat model.
- 🖥️ **Presets that use several graphics cards say so** — a preset that spreads the model over two or more cards keeps every card it names, says so in plain words on the desktop and the phone, and the memory estimate counts all of them.
- 🌙 **Free graphics memory when idle** — a new setting, off unless you pick 10, 30 or 60 minutes, unloads the model once KoboldCpp has had nothing to do for that long, so games and other programs can use the card. Its status says Unloaded until your next message, which loads it again by itself. Same on the phone.
- 🎛️ **KoboldCpp presets get a real editor** — "Generate KCPPS Config" is replaced by an editor for all your presets: open, rename, duplicate and delete them, and see how each one loads on your graphics card before you start it. It shows how much of the model goes on the card, the chat memory and the working space, and says in plain words when something won't fit, by how much, with a one-tap fix. You can now place layers and MoE experts by hand, with the memory worked out exactly. It also covers chat length, batch size, flash attention, MMQ (with a button that times both on your card), smart cache with a suggestion for your computer, the vision file and a draft model, plus a summary in plain words. "Save and use now" loads the preset straight into a running KoboldCpp. Because presets are for experts, a short pop-up asks first, and its main button keeps things automatic.
- 🏡 **A plain "Local model" card** — on the KoboldCpp settings page, auto mode now says how your model runs on this computer in plain words, and lets you pick how much chat history the character remembers. Each size gets a verdict: works like now, slower, or too big (with the most that works and a one-tap fix). Below 16,384 tokens is marked not recommended. A model file that is missing or can't be read is named as such. Same on the phone, on the Models page when chat runs on KoboldCpp, where you can also pick which KoboldCpp preset chat uses. While a preset sets the chat length, you change it in the preset, on the phone too.
- ⚙️ **Auto mode tunes itself to your computer**, from the very first start — it picks the largest batch size that keeps your model on the graphics card, keeps saved chats in memory when you have room so switching back is quick, and learns whether MMQ is faster on your NVIDIA or ROCm card from your own replies. The batch size in Advanced has a new **Auto** setting, now the default.
- 🔁 **A new preset or model loads straight away** — changing chat's preset or model in Settings while KoboldCpp runs now loads it in place, and so does switching model from the phone, instead of waiting for the next restart.
- 🛟 **A model that won't load no longer takes your working one down** — if KoboldCpp can't load the model or preset you just chose while another is running (a damaged or half-downloaded model file, say), it now keeps running the one you had and says so in plain words: the new model was not loaded, the previous one is still running, and why. Settings shows it in a message, the preset editor beside its buttons, and the phone on the model list and the Local model card, and every screen goes back to naming the model that is running, not the one that did not load. Before, the app stopped the working model, then refused to start the new one, and left you with nothing running and no reason.
- 🧭 **When KoboldCpp can't start, the app says why** — pressing Restart on the phone, or switching model there while KoboldCpp is stopped, now says why it didn't start (a half-downloaded model file, say) beside the buttons instead of just staying stopped. Opening a chat does the same: where the message box said only "No API connection", it now says the reason, on the desktop and above the box on the phone, until KoboldCpp runs.
- 🔧 **The tool-calling check no longer skips a model you switch to right after Start** — the sidebar's "Tool calling" check tests a model by itself as soon as it has loaded. If you picked another model or preset while that first check was still running, the new one was never checked and the pill stayed on "not tested". Now it is checked as soon as the first check ends, and a model that gives no answer is asked a few times instead of over and over.
- 🧠 **A model you already tested keeps its tool-calling answer** — the sidebar's "Tool calling" check now remembers, for each model, whether it can use tool calls, and keeps that after you close the app. A model you have tried is not tested again every time you open the app or start KoboldCpp; the pill shows the saved answer at once, on the computer and on the phone. Tap the pill to test again and replace the saved answer. A model that gives no answer is never saved, so it is simply tried again later. A model file is remembered by its name and size: move it to another folder and the answer stays, put a different file in its place and it is tested again.
- 🛟 **A model that won't load no longer takes your working one down** — if KoboldCpp can't load the model or preset you just chose while another is running (a damaged or half-downloaded model file, say), it now keeps running the one you had and says so in plain words: the new model was not loaded, the previous one is still running, and why. Settings shows it in a message, the preset editor beside its buttons, and the phone on the model list and the Local model card. Before, the app stopped the working model, then refused to start the new one, and left you with nothing running and no reason.
- ✂️ **Long chats keep their character card** — a chat set to a longer context than KoboldCpp runs with now builds its prompt to fit, instead of KoboldCpp quietly cutting the start (where the character card is).
- 📐 **The memory estimate is worked out from the model file** — the weights, the chat cache and the working memory are now read from the model file itself and KoboldCpp's own rules, and were checked against what a real KoboldCpp set aside for twenty models on Apple Silicon and on a 16 GB AMD card: the cache matches to the megabyte, and the working memory is never counted low. Before, the estimate missed sliding-window models (Gemma, gpt-oss) entirely, counted some caches twice, and could come out short at larger batch sizes.
- 🎯 **The model that loads is the model the app says is loaded** — every way of starting KoboldCpp (Settings, the model dialog, app start, opening a chat, the character creator, the phone) now follows one rule: the active preset's own model if it has one on this computer, otherwise the model you picked. Before, starting a preset that owned a different model left the status card, the vision check and the phone's "loaded" marker pointing at the old one. The character creator's model picker shows the model that loaded, too, and Settings names a preset's model by the same rule the launch uses.
- 📱 **Switching model from the phone uses the right settings** — the new model brings its own preset, or none. Before, the previous model's preset stayed active, so a bigger model started with the smaller one's context size.
- 🧳 **A preset from another computer still starts** — if a preset names a model file that isn't on this computer, the model you chose here is loaded with that preset's settings, and the engine log says so. A preset whose file was deleted is cleared instead of blocking the start.
- 🏠 **Settings opens on the model you last used** — the model list on Settings → Backend now starts on the model the Local model card names, not the first file in your models folder, so Start Backend loads the model you were running instead of quietly switching to another one.
- 🧷 **Start Backend no longer undoes your settings** — it launches exactly what is saved. A chat length you picked on the Local model card, set from the phone or got from a preset is kept, and **Reset to Automatic** stays automatic instead of turning into "CPU only", which left a graphics card unused. Same in the chat's Model Settings, where Start with Preset keeps the preset's chat length.
- 🔢 **GPU layers save as you type** — with "Set layers myself" on, the number is saved the moment you type it, on Settings and in the chat's Model Settings, so a restart, auto-start and the phone all use it. An emptied box no longer counts as 0 layers, and the chat length you type in Model Settings is saved as you type too.
- 🪞 **Model Settings and Advanced show what is saved** — after you clear a preset in the chat's Model Settings, the model list stays on the model that was running instead of jumping back to the first file in your folder, and the Context Window box on Settings → Advanced shows the chat length a preset, the Local model card or the phone set instead of the number it had when Settings opened.
- 🔗 **A preset is kept for the model it loads** — choosing a preset that names its own model (from the list, with Browse, or in the chat's Model Settings) now keeps it for that model, and Settings says which one. Before, it was kept for whichever model the list happened to show, so picking that model later turned the preset on and loaded the other one. Clearing a preset, with None or the X on its chip, clears it for the model it loaded. The phone follows the same rule.
- 📂 **A preset chosen with Browse loads straight away** — with KoboldCpp running, a preset picked with the folder button now loads in place, like one picked from the list, instead of only being chosen.
- 🔄 **Model swaps load the right model, every time** — when Realism checks or a story job use a different local model from chat, the app now sends KoboldCpp one request per swap and waits until the new model is really the one answering. Before, it sent "unload" and "load" back to back; KoboldCpp could drop the second, leaving no model loaded, and a reply could run on the old model for a moment.
- ♻️ **No pointless reloads** — a swap that would load what is already loaded sends nothing. A helper model set to the chat model's own model and preset never reloads, and a chat model you change in Settings in the middle of a story is the one put back afterwards.
- ⏱️ **Big models get the time they need** — after a swap or a restart the app waits in proportion to the model's size (a minute, plus eight seconds for every gigabyte) instead of giving up after ten seconds.
- 💬 **You can see which model is loading and why** — the status line reads, for example, "Loading model.gguf for the story...", and a swap that fails says what actually happened instead of always claiming chat was put back.
- 🪟 **Swaps no longer need links to model files** — the old swap had to link the model into KoboldCpp's admin folder, which stock Windows refuses to do. A swap now loads a small config the app writes there.
- 🔢 **Every message has a number** — shown under the avatar, starting at #1. The numbers on Journal and Growth Rings memories now match them (they used to start at #0), and tapping one lands on the start of that message instead of its middle. Same on the phone.
- 💬 **Your messages are laid out like the character's** — your picture on the left, your name on top, edit, fork and delete at the top right.
- 💞 **Generate reply now moves feelings and needs** — when your message is the last one (after deleting or stopping a reply, or forking at your line), Generate reply scores your message like a normal send, without counting it twice. Deleting the newest reply also undoes any quests it suggested. Group chats too, and the same on the phone.
- 🔀 **The model button in a phone chat works** — the model list shows and can be tapped, and you can switch provider right there (OpenRouter, Nano-GPT, xAI, LM Studio, KoboldCpp and more). Switching provider asks for your web login password, like Settings does.
- 💭 **Editing a message on the phone keeps its Thinking** — the editor's Thinking section now shows the reply's reasoning, and saving no longer erases it.
- 📜 **Reading old messages no longer snaps you down** — opening a Thought, scrolling inside one, or scrolling up through a long chat keeps you where you are. Same on the phone.

- ✏️ **Editing a message works on the phone again** — Save, Cancel and the text box answer taps, and on a notched iPhone the buttons sit below the clock. If a save fails, the editor stays open with your text and says why.
- 📱 **Phone taps that fail now say so** — regenerate, continue, swipe, fork, delete, switching chats, group settings and the rest show a plain reason instead of doing nothing.
- 📱 **A screen that breaks on the phone shows a way out** — Reload or Back to library, instead of a blank page.
- 📖 **Reading a story on the phone no longer overwrites it** — turning a page saves only your place, not an older copy of the story.
- 📱 **Leaving a chat mid-reply keeps that reply out of the next chat.**
- 📱 **With a chat theme picture, the Regenerate and version-picker windows cover the message box** like they do without one.
- ⚡ **Long chats on the phone stay smooth while a reply streams in.**
- ⌨️ **Japanese, Chinese and Korean keyboards no longer send mid-word** when Enter picks a character.
- 🖼️ **A photo the phone can't read is no longer dropped quietly** — your text and photo stay put with a note.
- 💡 **Suggested actions stay in the chat they were made for** — tapping Suggest actions and then opening another character no longer shows those suggestions (or the "Thinking…" spinner) under that chat's last message.

- 🤖 **xAI is a chat backend** — pick xAI in Settings → Backend (or Model Settings) and sign in with SuperGrok to use your subscription allowance instead of paid API credits. The sign-in is unofficial and at your own risk; an xAI API key also works. The sign-in borrows xAI's own Grok CLI login, so xAI may block it at any time. If your allowance runs out, chat says so in plain words. Same on the phone.

- 🧑 **A brand-new chat starts as your default persona** — left-clicking a character you've never opened now begins the chat with your default "Speak as", instead of carrying over the persona from the chat you were just in. Chats you've already opened keep their own persona. Same on the phone.
- 🖼️ **Saved ComfyUI edit workflows get the real prompt** — a chained or multi-step prompt now flows into your saved Edit workflow instead of being dropped, and Qwen image-edit keeps the denoise strength you set instead of snapping back to a default. Same on the phone.
- 🖼️ **Image before/after comparisons wait until both sides are done** — Image Studio no longer posts a half-finished ComfyUI comparison; an incomplete compare is skipped instead of shown broken.
- 🖼️ **ComfyUI workflows with Reroute dots keep every wire** — a saved workflow that passes a connection through a Reroute node no longer loses that connection when Image Studio loads it.
- 🏮 **Stoop card art keeps its head** — card pictures on The Stoop now crop from the top like the website does, so faces stay in frame on the browse grid, Mod's Picks and your uploads. World cards get a wide landscape frame, and a card's page shows the whole picture instead of a square cut.
- ⚡ **The Stoop loads its pictures fast** — the browse grid, Mod's Picks and your uploads now fetch the same small postcard images the website uses instead of every card's full-size original, keep them on disk so a second visit is instant, and fetch a few at a time instead of all at once. Opening a card shows the postcard right away while the full picture loads. Same on the phone.
