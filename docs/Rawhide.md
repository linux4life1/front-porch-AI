# Rawhide — What's New (Nightlies)

These notes are the in-app "Update Available" text for Rawhide builds. The
nightly takes every bullet under **Unreleased** as that build's release
body, and `tools/rotate-rawhide-notes.sh <tag>` then removes the bullets it
shipped, so a note is read here once and lives on in the GitHub release.
Write one bullet per change, on one line, with a bold title. Nothing else
goes in this file; `test/hygiene/rawhide_notes_test.dart` checks that.

Last shipped nightly: `nightly-rawhide.20261026.3de086a`

## Unreleased

- 💾 **Settings survive a crash** — On Windows and Linux a crash or power cut while saving could leave the settings file empty, and the app then quietly started over with default settings and no saved system prompts. Settings are now saved to a temporary file that replaces the old one only once it is complete, so the file on disk is always whole. The app also keeps a known-good copy, refreshed at launch, every 30 minutes and whenever you save or delete a prompt, and puts it back if the settings file is ever empty, missing or damaged; a notice says when the copy was from. On Windows the settings folder is now `%APPDATA%\Front Porch AI` instead of `%APPDATA%\com.antigravity\Front Porch AI`; the first launch copies the old one across and leaves it in place. A saved-prompt list that can't be read in full keeps every prompt it can and sets the rest aside instead of being replaced. On the desktop app.
- 🎨 **UI Settings says where your bubble colours come from in a group** — Opened on a group member, UI Settings now notes under User Bubble and User Text that in a group your bubbles use your own colours from Settings, so the member's own user colours only show in their 1:1 chats. On the desktop.
- 🖼️ **Group members wear their starred mood picture** — In a group, a member whose mood has no picture of its own now shows the picture starred in their character's gallery, as their 1:1 chats do, instead of the first picture in the set. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🧠 **Porch Life decides whether Realism can run** — The Realism Engine switch in Settings → Porch Life now allows Realism for every chat instead of starting new chats with it; that job moved to the switch nested under it, "Start every new chat with Realism". With Realism Engine off, no chat runs Realism (bonds, mood, Needs and Afterglow pause), and each chat's own Realism switch (in Character State, or Group Settings → Realism for a group) greys out with a note that points to Porch Life and keeps that chat's choice for when you turn it back on. Turning it back on asks whether to switch Realism on in the chats that have it off. A 1:1 chat that has never run Realism reads where things stand the first time it runs; a chat that ran it before picks up where it left off. A Realism Engine change made while a reply is on its way waits for that reply to finish. Standing Mood now follows whether Needs is running. On the desktop and the web app.
- 👥 **New groups start each member from their character card** — A new group (the new-group wizard, the phone's New Group, and a group made from a 1:1 with /join --full, /promote or Step out to direct) now starts each member's bonds, trust, mood and Needs where their card starts a 1:1 chat, with anything you set in the wizard on top; before, every member started at the same neutral values. A card with no Realism settings starts at a bond and trust of 0, as its 1:1 chats do. "Reset to character defaults" goes back to the card, Neutral and High Bond set the bonds and mood and keep the card's Needs, and a member you never touched starts from their card. Turning Realism or Needs on for a group's new chats later starts members without saved starting values from their cards too. Groups you already have keep their saved starting values. A quick double click on Create no longer makes two groups. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🗣️ **Group members keep their own voice and job** — In a group, two members who share a name now each speak in their own voice, and a member whose library character was renamed (or whose name another character took) keeps their voice, their "At work" tag and the job the chat is told about, as well as their pictures and Pockets. A guest who joins a 1:1 chat speaks in their own voice instead of the host's. On the phone, the 🔊 on a line plays in that speaker's voice (a 1:1 used to play the Settings voice), and System banners have no 🔊. On the desktop and the web app.
- 👥 **Each group member's card shows what's running for them** — In a group chat a member's card now shows their own recovery countdown under their Lust while Afterglow runs for them, their Ambitions and Pockets even with the group's Realism off, and a portrait that follows that member's own mood instead of one mood for the whole chat. The phone's group panel shows the same for the member it is about, including their own Lust and recovery when no member is picked. Mood pictures ask the model only while Expressions is on and the member has pictures, and a word the model can't place costs one question per chat. With Expressions off, the phone shows the character's picture instead of a mood picture, as the desktop does. On the desktop and the web app.
- ♡ **Needs has its chat switch back** — Each 1:1 chat has its own Needs switch again, on the Needs line in Character State and in the phone's chat tools; group chats keep theirs in Group Settings → Needs. While Settings → Porch Life has Needs off, the switch greys out with a note that points to Porch Life, and the chat keeps its choice. Editing a character's Needs switch now only sets how their new chats start; chats you already have keep their own. Turning Porch Life Needs on asks whether to turn it on in the chats that have it off. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🔥 **Afterglow has its chat switch back** — Afterglow in Settings → Porch Life (with 18+ on) now allows Afterglow instead of running it in every chat, and each chat has its own switch again: on the Afterglow line in Character State, in Group Settings → Realism for a group chat, and in the chat tools on the phone. A character's card (creator and editor) and a group (Edit group, the new-group wizard, and New chats in the phone's Group Settings) set how their new chats start it. When Porch Life starts allowing Afterglow (its switch, or 18+ themes), a short question offers to turn it on in the 1:1 and group chats that have it off; its switch starts off, so nothing changes unless you flip it, and only those chats' switches change. The phone's realism panel now shows Lust only while Afterglow runs in that chat. On the desktop and the web app.
- ✏️ **Edit a group member in full** — Edit Character, opened on a group member from the chat sidebar or from Edit group, now edits that member in this group with every section: Ambitions, work, birthday, likes, Pockets, Recovery, Needs and the member's own Afterglow switch. A line at the top says whose copy it is, and their library card and other chats stay as they are. The group's own settings (Realism, time and day, Chaos, Needs and Afterglow) show as read-only lines that say where each one is set, and the member's starting bonds, trust and mood set where they start in the group's new chats. Group Settings and Edit group list members as short summaries that open it. The web app gets the same editor: Edit in a group chat opens it for the member you are looking at, and Group Settings has an Edit link for each member. Save on Edit Character ignores a second tap while it saves, and a save that fails says so and lets you try again. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🔥 **Afterglow off keeps Lust** — switching a chat's or a group member's Afterglow off now only hides their Lust and recovery; switching it back on brings both back where they were, instead of starting from zero. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🎬 **Director authority on the desktop** — Edit Character's Realism tab on the desktop now has the Director authority switch for a character's verifier, as the web app already did.
- 🧪 **A group's Needs switch follows Porch Life** — while Needs is off in Porch Life, Group Settings → Needs, Edit group's Needs switch and the phone's group Needs control grey out with the same note as a one-on-one chat's, and keep the group's own choice. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🔥 **Each group member has their own Afterglow switch** — In Group Settings → Realism each member's card has an Afterglow switch, and the phone's chat tools list one per member, while the group chat's own switch is on. Switched off, Afterglow stops for that member in this group; the rest of the cast carry on. Members join a group with their own switch on, whatever their card says about new 1:1 chats. The sidebar and the phone show a member's Lust only while Afterglow runs for them. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🔥 **Each group member's Recovery block** — How a member comes down after climax can now be set for them in the group: under their Afterglow switch in Group Settings → Realism, in their member editor and in the phone's Group Settings, while Afterglow runs for them. It is saved on that member in this group; their library card keeps its own. On the desktop and the web app.
- 💾 **Edits to a group member stick** — in a group chat, Edit Character and UI Settings from the sidebar now save to that member, and Edit group's member Edit now saves the voice and greeting moods it shows. Before, those edits were gone the next time the group opened. On the desktop.
- 💞 **Long-Term Bond stays Long-Term Bond in groups** — in the new-group wizard (desktop and web app) and Edit group (desktop), the Long-Term Bond slider sets each member's long-term bond instead of their trust, and Trust Level sets only trust. Before, both sliders wrote trust, so a new group's members could start with a trust far past its scale. In a group saved that way with a Long-Term Bond beyond Trust Level's scale, Edit group shows it as Long-Term Bond again and keeps it once you move a slider.
- 🎙️ **The new-group wizard keeps the voice you pick** — a Voice chosen for a member on the wizard's Members step now goes with that member into the group, and "Use the global voice" means the member follows Settings. Before, the group kept the character's own voice and the pick landed on the library character instead, where their one-on-one chats used it and the next save of that character kept it. The web app's wizard gets the same per-member Voice on its Details step. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🧹 **Deleting a world deletes its places** — deleting a world in Worlds now deletes every place inside it too, instead of leaving them behind as loose worlds in the library, and the confirm says how many places go with it. Deleting a place takes the rooms inside it. Places left loose by an earlier delete are ordinary worlds now and can be deleted by hand. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🌍 **Characters and groups link whole worlds** — the Worlds tab of the character editor, group settings, Edit group and the new-group wizard list only worlds now, each with how many places it holds and where its chats start; a place is reached through its world. Linking a place on its own used to start chats with no Setting and no weather. A character or group that already links a place keeps that link: its new chats start at that place with the world's lore and weather, and the picker shows the world as linked at that place. A chat that started without a Setting because of it gets that place as its Setting once. On the desktop, and in the web app's character editor.
- ⛈️ **Weather you can feel** — with Ambient scene set to Tint and effects, the weather over the chat draws with depth now: rain falls as streaks with soft tails in three layers, with a far sheet behind them (and a third sheet in a storm), leaning and stretching in gusts, lit by every lightning strike, and throwing a ring and two droplets where each near drop lands; a storm flash strikes, goes dark and strikes again, with a forked lightning bolt down from the top; fog drifts past as layers of wispy mist, thicker toward the ground, instead of a flat grey wash; snow has a soft halo on the nearest flakes and pinpoint flakes far behind; clouds have a lit top and drift with a slow bob. Same numbers, same seed, same scene on desktop and web; Reduce motion still holds it all still.
- 🏞️ **The chat shows where the story is** — a chat whose Setting is a place with a picture now paints that picture behind the bubbles, under the story's weather, in place of the chosen background; a place with no picture of its own shows its town's, and a world with none keeps your built-in or custom background. When the scene moves (the Places breadcrumb, or an accepted Scene suggestion), the weather plays the move: fog rolls over the chat and clears on the new place, a storm cuts there on a lightning strike, and anywhere else the old place drifts off under a sweep of light while the new one settles, with the place's name on a card for a moment. Reduce motion swaps at once and still names the place. Same on the web chat page.
- ⭐ **Every world has a place where its chats start** — chats that get a world (from a character or group that carries it, or attached as the Setting in the Places panel) now begin in a place inside it, not in the world at large, still with the world's lore and weather. It is set in the Worlds menu: New World asks you to name the starting place, which is made inside the new world; Edit World has a Where chats start picker among its places; and every place card in the library has a star, like the star that picks a character's default portrait, to move it with one tap. World from Wiki and World from Character ask on their Review step (pick a place the run wrote, or name a new one) and Mint waits until you do; the starter worlds come with one set. An older world with places asks on its card until you star one. Add as lore still adds only the world's books, Move the scene still takes you to the world itself, and the starting place travels with an exported world. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🖼️ **The starter worlds come with pictures** — all seven starter worlds and every place inside them now carry a painted cover of their own, so the Worlds grid shows the frozen Whitereach, the gas-giant sky over Kestrel Reach and the lantern-lit market in Harborside, and a chat set in a starter place paints that place behind the bubbles from the first message. Each is an ordinary world picture: change it or remove it in Edit World like any other. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🗺️ **Worlds stay a grid of worlds** — in Worlds, only worlds get cards now; the places inside a world fold into it. A world with places says how many and where chats start, and tapping that opens its places as a tidy list right under its row: star where chats start, add a place inside, edit, move or delete, with rooms indented under their building. The header counts worlds, not places, and Roots only is gone. Export is on worlds only and always saves the whole world with every place in it, so a shared file is never missing places or its starting place. On the desktop and the web app.
- ⏩ **Time skips read like you meant them** — when your message moves time ("we slept eight hours and woke up", "two weeks later", "skip to 7:30"), a short model read decides how far and where the clock lands, in place of the old phrase rules that got some lines wrong (eight hours from 1:40 AM now lands at 9:40, not 8:00). End any message with `/skip 8h`, `/skip 2d`, `/skip morning` or `/skip 07:30` for an exact skip, or send `/skip …` alone to move the clock without a message. A night everyone slept through wakes the whole cast rested in groups too, and skips of three hours or more are named for the next reply. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🕰️ **The chat asks when a skip is unclear** — when a message moves time and the read is shaky (it could mean two things, it named no amount, the read failed, or it moves the story a day or more), a "Did time pass?" question opens before the reply: the best guess is picked, the other readings sit beside it with where each lands, and you can pick No skip or type your own (like `8h` or `morning`). Closing it keeps the guess. It only asks while someone has the chat open, so Director auto-chat and AFK use the best guess. A bottom sheet on the phone; on the desktop and the web app.
- 📖 **Porch Stories can be set in a world** — New Story's Idea step has a third card, Where does it happen?, that picks a world from your Worlds library or a place inside one; the story starts there and reads the whole world. The bible is written against the world's places, climate and book instead of inventing a town of its own, the planner sets every scene in one of the world's places (or names a new one inside it, marked new place on the Structure page), and the writer sees the place's own description and that place's facts first. The world's facts are copied into Lore & continuity with a from-world chip, with Refresh from world and Detach on a header card; the shelf, the Overview and the header name the world. A story made from a chat that has a Setting is set there, and Write a story here on a world card or a place row in Worlds opens New Story set there. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🌙 **Skips the reply writes count in full** — when a character's reply jumps hours ("six hours later, the rain stops"), the clock now moves the whole span instead of stopping at three hours, and when the reply carries everyone through a night, the cast wakes rested, in groups too, the same as a night you skip yourself. A "three days later, that evening" landing works with every backend now, not only some. On the desktop and the web app.
- 👥 **A group chat can become a Porch Story** — Turn Into a Story… in the chat menu now works in a group chat, with a Lead row to pick who the story follows (the first member unless you say otherwise; you can lead it yourself), and New Story's Choose a chat… lists group chats with their roster, searchable by any member's name. Every member joins the cast with their card, the first as Protagonist and the rest as Supporting, with role chips on the Cast step; the chat's timeline names who said what by each message's speaker, and the bible is told to keep every character as themselves. A group chat with a Setting puts the story in that world, as a 1:1 chat does. On the desktop and the web app, where the chat tools' Turn this chat into a story gets the same Lead pick.
- 🪟 **Window buttons show in Windows Light mode** — the minimize, maximize and close buttons could be white on a white title bar in Windows Light mode; the title bar is now an ordinary Windows one. It also recolours straight away when you switch Windows between Light and Dark with Front Porch open, instead of keeping its old colours until a restart.
- 🧭 **A group meets in one world, at the place you pick** — the new-group wizard has a Setting step: pick one world, and new chats start at its starred place or at any other place inside it you pick, with "Also read lore from" for worlds that should add their lorebook without their weather. Edit group and the in-chat group settings use the same picker, so a group can no longer link a second world whose weather and starting place were silently ignored. A group that already links several worlds opens with today's split filled in and keeps starting new chats exactly as before; chats already started keep their worlds. The wizard's step dots and Back button no longer skip Opening in groups of five or more. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🔀 **A group made from a 1:1 keeps its settings** — adding someone to a 1:1 chat now saves the new group with that chat's Realism, Needs, Chaos and world for its later chats, and keeps reading the original character's lorebook; Edit group used to show them all off. A group made this way before opens Edit group with an offer to fill them in from the chat it came from, and nothing changes until you save. The web app's new groups start with Realism and Needs on, as on the desktop.
- 📱 **The web app's new-group wizard catches up** — on the phone, making a group now walks the same seven steps as the desktop: Members, Details (with Auto-Advance and Director Mode), Setting, Realism (the group switches, Group Chaos, the story's start date and time, the Neutral and High Bond presets, and each member's bond, trust, emotion, verification and needs, plus Group Dynamics for four or fewer), Opening (first message, alternate greetings and scenario, with Generate writing the scene where the group meets, with its Group Dynamics), Prompts & Lore (per-member prompts, the group lorebook and Inherit), and Review, with a Change link on every line. Generate on the desktop now says "with Dynamics" only when there are some to send.
- 🎬 **Group openings are written where the group meets** — in the new-group wizard, Generate for the scenario and first message now writes the scene at the place the Setting step picked, with that place and its world's description, the weather and the world's always-on lore, and the Opening step says where that is. The wizard is seven steps in a fixed order — Members, Identity, Setting, Realism (with Group Dynamics inside it for groups of four or fewer), Opening, Prompts & Lore, Review. Next and Back never skip a step, and Generate on Opening sees the Group Dynamics you set.
- 📁 **Extracted characters stay in the group's folder** — Extract Characters on a group that lives in a folder now puts the new character cards in that same folder, right beside the group, instead of on Home. A group on Home still extracts to Home. On the desktop and the web app.
- 🗺️ **A character meets in one world, at the place you pick** — a character's Worlds tab, on the desktop and the web, now uses the same picker as groups: one world sets the weather and where new chats start, you can pick another place inside it, and "Also read lore from" adds worlds for their lore only. A character that already links several worlds keeps starting chats exactly as before, with that split filled in and a note explaining it; chats you've already started keep their worlds.
- 🔁 **Groups made from a 1:1 say which chat** — Edit group's offer to fill in a forked group's settings now names the character the chat was with, lists what it would fill in, and has Leave as is beside Use the chat's settings. The web app's Group settings get the same offer, plus Realism, Needs and Inherit switches for the group's new chats.
- 🎭 **No group member stays the main character** — once a member had answered in a group chat, the app kept them as the chat's active character for the rest of the visit: Chance Time named them whoever was speaking, and your messages took their text colour and font, during every turn as well. Now nobody stays active. Chance Time names the member who answers; in random turn order the spin picks them ahead, and an Away member you call by name still comes back. Your messages keep your own colours and font, and a group chat no longer shows one member's expression picture behind it, as on the phone. Steps for a member's goal are written for that member, and with nobody picked the web app's goals and pockets panels belong to the first member, as its header shows. On the desktop and the web app.
