# Rawhide — What's New (Nightlies)

These notes feed the in-app "Update Available" dialog for Rawhide / cutting-edge builds.

## Recent improvements (unreleased — ships in the next build)

- 🎭 **Emotional Voice — all in one sidecar, zero conflicts** — the emotional DSP that adapts TTS to your character's mood now lives inside `kokoro_tts.py` itself (gated behind `--emotional`), eliminating the separate `emotional_voice.py` sidecar. The TTS code path is completely unaffected: numpy/scipy import only inside the emotional handler, not at module level. Works across all four TTS engines (Kokoro, Piper, OpenAI, ElevenLabs). Opt in via Settings → Voice & Media → Emotional Voice. Defaults off.

- 🎭 **Expression packs — give any character a full set of matching emotion portraits in one sitting** — in the Image Studio, pick your character and hit **Expression pack…**: choose a Starter (8 emotions) or Full (28 emotions) set, and the Studio paints every expression from one base portrait so they all look like the *same person* — same face, same style, just a different feeling on it. Watch each image appear in a live grid as it generates; keep the ones you love, re-roll the ones you don't, cancel and resume any time. One tap imports them all as labeled expression images and switches character expressions on. Works for your 1:1 character and for any picked group member. (Needs a local image backend — A1111, ComfyUI, or Draw Things.)
- 👁️ **The AI can now double-check your expression pack** — after a pack generates, hit **Vision check**: the model compares each image against the base portrait — same person? does the emotion read? — and marks every one with a green ✓ or an amber ⚠ you can hover for the reason, plus a one-tap "Uncheck flagged" to drop the misses before importing. Purely advisory — you always keep the final say. (Needs a text model that can see images: a vision-capable local GGUF with its mmproj loaded, or a vision model on your remote API.)
- 💞 **Characters recover naturally after intimacy — no more permanently cold partners** — with the NSFW cooldown on, a character who climaxed could come out of their recovery period *stuck*: every message scored their desire further down, and they'd read as repulsed by their own lover indefinitely. Three real bugs fixed (the engine told the model a cooldown was active forever, "sated" was being treated as "disgusted," and in group chats the recovery timer never counted down at all). The whole experience is more human now: right after, they're blissed-out and affectionate — not cold; while recovering, advances get a warm "not yet" instead of a rejection that poisons the mood; and when the recovery ends, desire actually returns to normal. Genuine turn-offs (unwanted advances, betrayals) still sour things — that part is supposed to hurt. Applies to 1:1 and group chats equally.
- 🎨 **The Image Studio is simpler now — just pick what you're picturing** — instead of a confusing row of five "generation types," the Studio opens on one clean canvas with a **Subject** at the top: *Freeform* (type anything), the *character* you're chatting with, or *your persona*. Pick a character or persona and the prompt auto-fills from their appearance, ready to tweak. Everything technical — backend, model, size, steps, sampler, scheduler, seed, LoRA — tucks into a collapsible **Generation Settings** panel, so the everyday view stays focused on making the picture. Your reference-image (img2img) and denoise controls are right on the canvas. (Two rarely-loved modes retired in the cleanup: the standalone "Visualize Scene" — its magic lives on as Freeform-blank-then-Craft, which still paints the current scene — and "Chat Background" generation; you still set a chat background from the Background settings.) — the Image Studio now takes an optional reference image: drop one in, and instead of starting from scratch it paints your prompt *over* your image. A Denoise slider appears only once you've added a reference — slide it low to keep the original close, high to let the prompt take over. Works on all three local backends (Automatic1111, ComfyUI, and Draw Things), and there's now a single denoise control instead of the old Draw-Things-only strength knob.
- 🧩 **The Image Studio stops you from picking a LoRA that can't work** — a LoRA only works on a checkpoint from the same model family (an SD1.5 LoRA on an SDXL checkpoint just makes noise or errors out). The Studio now knows each LoRA's family — read straight from the file's own metadata on Automatic1111 and ComfyUI, or from the file name on Draw Things — and lines it up against your selected checkpoint. LoRAs it's *certain* won't work are tucked into a one-tap "Show N incompatible" drawer; ones it's only guessing about (or the Pony-on-SDXL soft case) stay in the list with a small "likely" tag; and anything it can't identify is always shown, so nothing good ever disappears. Pick a mismatched one anyway and you get a plain-words heads-up before you generate. Each LoRA now wears its family as a little badge (SDXL ✓, SD1.5, Flux…).

- 🧹 **Mass-delete characters (finally) — for when an import goes sideways** — accidentally imported a hundred cards you didn't want? Enter select mode, tick the ones to remove, and hit "Delete N Selected" to wipe them all at once (cards, images, and chat histories). Deleting a folder now also offers "Delete Folder + Characters" to nuke everything inside — subfolders included — in one go. Because these are irreversible and can hit a lot at once, both actions make you **type DELETE** to confirm first. Works the same on desktop and the web/mobile app.
- 🔧 **See at a glance if your model supports tool calling** — a new pill at the top of the chat sidebar (and the web app's insight panel) tells you whether the model you're running can answer the engine's evaluations with native tool calls: green "supported", amber "not supported — using text fallback" (everything still works, structured results are just a bit less reliable), or "not tested" with tap-to-test. It re-checks automatically whenever you switch models or backends, so the verdict is always about the model you're actually running. On OpenRouter and Nano-GPT the answer now comes straight from the provider's own model catalog — instant and free, no test message sent to the model; local models and other servers keep the quick test, and tap-to-retest always runs the real thing. Bonus fix: a temporarily unreachable backend no longer gets wrongly branded "no tool support" for the rest of the session.
- 🏡 **Porch Stories got a full warm-porch makeover** — the whole story area now matches the rest of the app (the old blue-grey look is gone, light mode finally works properly), and creating a story is a proper step-by-step wizard: Engine → Concept → Style → Format → Cast → Review, just like the character creator. No more one giant scrolling form.
- 🔌 **Stories now show your AI engine — and let you fix it** — a new AI Engine card sits right in the story library, the story dashboard, and step one of the new-story wizard: it shows which backend and model will write your story, whether it's ready, and a Set Up button that opens the same model picker chat uses. If a generation fails because the engine is down, the error now includes a "Set Up AI" button. Same on the web app, with a one-tap Start.
- 🎛️ **Story generation tunes itself per step** — planning steps (outlines, scene lists, beat plans) now run precise and disciplined while prose writing runs warm and creative, instead of everything sharing one setting. Small local models keep their structure intact; prose gets more life. No dials — it's just better out of the box.
- 🏷️ **"Prompt style" no longer pretends to be a model picker** — the old "Large Local Models (70B+)" choice in story setup sounded like it selected your model (it never did — it only changed how prompts are written). It's now clearly labeled "Prompt style" with plain descriptions, and the real model lives in the AI Engine card right above it.
- 📖 **Stories now tell you what they need** — kicking off a story stage without your AI engine running used to fail with a scary technical error ("OS Error: The remote computer refused the network connection"). Now Porch Stories checks first and says it plainly: stories use the same AI backend as chat — start your engine and load a model (or set up your remote API), then try again. If the engine goes away mid-write you get the same plain-words message instead of raw network jargon. Applies on desktop and the web app.
- 💛 **Warm characters stay warm — and trust finally feels like it moves** — with Realism on, an open, funny, easygoing character could come across stiff and guarded even on a first meeting, and trust felt almost impossible to earn. Three fixes: the engine no longer quietly tells the model to hold back a character's natural warmth and humor at low trust — trust now only controls how far they let you *in*, not their basic temperament, so a warm character reads warm from turn one and a prickly one stays prickly. Genuinely good moments now bank a dependable bit of trust instead of often scoring nothing. And the early trust label reads "Warming" instead of the old "Cautious," so you can actually see progress the moment it starts. Applies to 1:1, groups, and web/mobile alike.
- 🖥️ **Older PCs are supported now (no AVX2? no problem)** — the AI engine's standard download needs a newer CPU feature (AVX2) and would crash on launch on older or budget machines, leaving those users stuck. Front Porch AI now detects a non-AVX2 CPU on Windows and Linux and automatically grabs KoboldCpp's compatible "old PC" build instead — so it just works, no manual downloads or guesswork. And if that machine also has no NVIDIA GPU (so it can't use graphics acceleration at all), you'll now get a clear heads-up that local AI will run on the CPU and be slow — with a nudge toward a cloud model — instead of wondering why it's crawling.
- 🔥 **NSFW mode is now easy to find in group chats** — the NSFW Enhancements toggle (arousal / Lust bar + post-climax cooldowns) now lives right in Group Settings → Realism, under the master Realism switch, so you can enable it for the whole group where you'd expect to. It was previously only tucked behind the sidebar gear. On web/mobile the same toggle now correctly reflects the group-wide state instead of the last speaker's.
- 🧼 **"Prefers being dirty" now behaves — and stops leaking** — two fixes to the enjoys-low-hygiene trait. (1) The preference could leak between characters: after a 1:1 with a musk-loving character, a totally different character in a group could suddenly be written as feeling disgustingly filthy even at high, clean hygiene. It's now strictly per-character — a clean character stays clean and reads as comfortable. (2) For a character who genuinely loves being unwashed, being scrubbed clean is now described correctly — restless, exposed, itching to get grubby and smell like herself again — instead of the old backwards "she feels filthy" wording. 1:1 chats with normal characters were never affected.
- 🌱 **Growth Rings: settled characters keep visibly growing** — in very long chats, a character who had built up eight or more strong permanent rings could quietly stop *showing* new growth: fresh habits and stances were still recorded on the timeline, but the strongest old rings hogged all the character's attention, forever. Two of those attention slots are now always saved for the newest in-progress growth, so even a set-in-their-ways character keeps evolving on screen. Also fixed: the AI could retire a ring you had pinned — pinned now truly means permanent (only you can retire a pinned ring, from the Growth timeline). Applies to 1:1, groups, and the web/mobile app alike.
- 🎆 **Expression emoji burst** — When expressions are enabled and the character's mood changes, a flurry of matching emojis springs from the avatar badge and floats outward before popping away. Opt in via Settings → Voice & Media → Expression Emoji Burst, and drag the **Burst emoji size** slider to pick how big the particles are.
- 🎭 **Emotional Voice — TTS adapts to your character's mood** — With Expressions on, TTS playback is subtly shaped by how the character is feeling: joy brightens and lifts the voice, sadness slows and darkens it, anger adds edge and tension, surprise sharpens the pitch. Uses pure-Dart STFT spectral processing (no Python, no external dependencies) to modify pitch contour, brightness, breathiness, and speaking rate per emotion. Works across all four TTS engines (Kokoro, Piper, OpenAI, ElevenLabs). Opt in via Settings → Voice & Media → Emotional Voice. Defaults off.
- ☕ **Your character keeps living while you're away (AFK)** — turn on **Dynamic Responses** (Settings → Generation) and, once you've stepped away from an open chat, your character quietly gets on with their day instead of just waiting: up to three short solitary snapshots — a meal, a nap, a shower, some reading — arrive on a timer you set (30 seconds to 5 minutes apart). Time drifts forward and their Needs move to match what they actually did — a shower lifts hygiene, a nap restores energy, a meal fills hunger — with the usual needs chip on each auto-message so you can see what changed. It never talks *to* you or breaks the scene, waits until you've traded at least one message, stops after three so it can't run away, and resets cleanly the moment you type or switch chats. Prefer to keep the engines off? It still works — you just get the quiet narration, no clock or needs. You can also flip it on straight from the message box: type **/afk** (or **/afk --messages 3 --time 5m** to send three, five minutes apart), and **/afk off** to stop.
- 📔 **Characters actually keep a diary now (especially on "thinking" models)** — The Journal was quietly writing its "Where we are" recap every chat but almost never jotting down the individual memories that make it feel alive, so most chats showed an empty diary. Two things were fixed: (1) the character is now told to capture what mattered — every promise, thing it learned about you, relationship shift, and moment that stood out — first, before the recap; and (2) on reasoning/"thinking" models (e.g. Kimi K2.6:thinking via OpenRouter/Nano-GPT), the journal update now runs with thinking switched off. Previously the model would burn its whole response budget silently "thinking" and get cut off before it ever wrote the entries — so entries appeared only intermittently (you'd have to regenerate two or three times). Now it writes them the first time, faster, and captures more of them.

- 📡 **The Stoop is alive now — upvotes and downloads tick up in real time** — when someone upvotes or downloads a character while you're browsing The Stoop, you see the number change *the moment it happens*: on the browse grid, the featured banner, an open character panel, creator profiles, even your past-downloads row. No more refreshing or hopping out and back in to see how a card is doing. Post a new character on Discord and watch the downloads roll in live.

- 💾 **Fixed: your character's Needs sometimes "rewound" after closing the app** — occasionally, closing and reopening Front Porch AI would snap the Needs bars back to where they were *before* the last reply, as if that message's changes (the little "Bladder +85, Fun +18…" chips) never happened. The app was closing a beat too fast, before that last save finished writing to disk. It now waits for every pending save to fully land before it shuts down, so the state you see when you close is exactly the state you get when you reopen.

- 🪟 **Fixed: app opening as a stuck taskbar icon you couldn't restore (Windows 11)** — if Front Porch AI ever launched to nothing but an icon on the taskbar that wouldn't maximize (and reinstalling didn't help), this is fixed. Closing the app while it was minimized could save a bogus off-screen window position that then got reloaded on the next launch, parking the window where you couldn't reach it. It now refuses to restore a window that wouldn't land on any of your monitors and recenters instead — which also repairs any install that was already stuck, and rescues windows left off-screen after unplugging a second monitor.

- 🎛️ **Your sampler settings actually reach the model now** — if you ever felt like moving Min-P, Repeat Penalty, XTC, or Dynamic Temperature did nothing… you were right. Only Temperature was truly being delivered; the rest were silently dropped on the way to the model (and Repeat Penalty was being translated into a much weaker lookalike). Every slider now arrives for real, on local models and remote APIs alike. And while we were in there, three new knobs joined the panel: **Top-P** and **Top-K** (work everywhere), and **DRY** — the modern anti-repetition sampler that catches repeated *phrases*, not just words (local models; try 0.8 if your character keeps recycling the same lines). Heads-up: because Min-P and the real Repeat Penalty now genuinely apply, replies may feel slightly different — that's the sliders finally doing their job.

- 🛋️ **The chat sidebar got a complete warm-porch makeover** — nine mismatched, bolted-on sections became three tidy groups: **🎭 Character State** (their mood, bond, trust, needs, and the time of day in one card), **📖 Journal & Memory**, and **🎲 Story Tools** (author's note, objectives, chaos, lorebook). The big fix: **Lust is no longer quarantined in its own orange box** — it's a bar right under Bond and Trust, same look, same place, in solo *and* group chats, with the refractory countdown tucked beneath it (NSFW on/off now lives in the little gear next to it). Every bar in the app finally uses one consistent style, the whole sidebar respects light mode properly for the first time, collapsed cards show a one-line vital sign ("Fond · Trusting · Evening" — with a 🧠 when they're fixated), and the sidebar remembers which groups you keep open between launches. **Author's Note sits right at the top** as its own card (with an "active · strength N" hint when one is set), flipping Realism on now pops the stats open so you instantly see what you enabled, and the **Automatic Passage of Time toggle actually works now** — it turns out it was quietly broken before this redesign too: the switch changed a value that was never saved or repainted, which is also why it seemed to snap back on (or get "forced on" after touching One-Shot Eval). It now saves properly from both the sidebar and Group Settings.
- 🖌️ **Watch your image come to life** — generating is no longer a black box. A live card in the chat (and in the Image Studio, and the web app) shows the picture actually forming: A1111 and ComfyUI stream the in-progress frame as it sharpens, Draw Things shows a moving step bar, and remote APIs show honest activity. No more staring at a frozen spinner wondering if anything is happening.
- 🎁 **Image generation is on out of the box** — the ✨ button and /image are right there from first launch (nothing generates until you pick a backend, so there's zero overhead if you never use it). The old hidden toggle in Settings still works if you want it gone.
- ✍️ **See the prompt before it generates** — when `/image` crafts a prompt for you, a review card now pops up first so you can read it, tweak it, or cancel — on desktop and the web app. If you'd rather it just go, flip off "Review AI prompts before generating" in the image settings.
- 🔗 **Server addresses forgive missing http://** — typing `localhost:8188` or `192.168.1.20:7860` for a local image backend now just works instead of silently failing until you remembered the `http://` prefix.
- 🕸️ **ComfyUI support — no node graphs required** — pick ComfyUI as your image backend and Front Porch finds it automatically (it checks the standard address the moment you select it — no Test button, no URLs to type for a default install). Your checkpoints, LoRAs, and samplers appear in the same simple dropdowns as every other backend, and generation uses a built-in workflow behind the scenes — you never touch a node graph. Works from the Image Studio, /image in chat, portraits, and the web app.
- 🔌 **Image backend setup stopped being fiddly** — the Image Studio's generation settings now check your connection automatically when you open them or switch backends, and one friendly status card tells you what's happening: "Draw Things connected — 12 models · 4 LoRAs", or plain-words help like "Is ComfyUI running? It listens on http://127.0.0.1:8188 by default." The scattered Test buttons are gone; models, LoRAs, and samplers just fill in when the server is found.
- 🎨 **Generate images right in the chat with /image** — type `/image` and the current scene is painted for you, appearing as a picture bubble in the conversation (works in 1:1 and group chats, on desktop and the web app). Variants: `/image me` (your persona), `/image char [name]` (a character portrait), `/image raw <prompt>` (send your exact prompt to the backend), or just `/image a dragon over the castle` for anything you can describe. The `/` helper panel lists it, and `/img`, `/sd`, and `/imagine` work too.
- 🖼️ **Chat images are real images now** — right-click any generated picture in chat to Save it anywhere on your computer, reveal it in your file manager, or copy the prompt that made it; click to zoom. The Image Studio gained a "Send to chat" button so a result you love lands straight in the conversation, and the web app's "Insert into chat" now shows up properly on desktop too instead of as raw text.
- 🧬 **Draw Things finally has LoRA support** — pick a LoRA (fetched straight from your Draw Things install) and set its strength in the Image Studio's generation settings, exactly like A1111. It's applied natively through the Draw Things engine, so no prompt-tag hacks. Portrait generations also now come out portrait-oriented instead of inheriting a landscape default size.
- 🛠️ **Every image path now plays by the same rules** — character-creator avatars (desktop and web) no longer force a tiny 512×512 (which some cloud models flat-out rejected) and use your configured size instead; Image Studio portraits orient vertically like every other portrait; and your default negative prompt now applies everywhere it can — auto-generated guest portraits, creator avatars, and the web app's image panel included, not just the Studio.
- 🎨 **One place for all theme and color settings.** The per-chat theme picker (preset, font, border, colors) and the per-character UI settings (bubble colors, dialogue, actions) are now in a single **UI Settings** dialog — no more hunting between two separate dialogs. When a theme is active, color changes save to the theme; when no theme is set, they save to the character. Dialogue and action colors are now part of every theme preset, so they change along with the rest of the look when you switch themes. Steampunk's gear border is also lighter and scales better with different bubble sizes.
- 📖 **Turn any chat into a book.** A beloved roleplay can now become a keepsake: pick **Turn Into a Story…** from the chat menu, choose faithful retelling or inspired-by, short story or novella, and your point of view — and Front Porch builds a ready-to-run Porch Stories project scoped to *that chat*. The pipeline distills your actual conversation into a canon timeline, drafts the story scene by scene (in faithful mode it follows your real events in order — no invented plot), and exports an eBook when you're happy. Also available on the web app: one tap under chat tools → "Our story".
- 💍 **"Remember our wedding vows?" — now they actually do, word for word.** Ask a character about a moment from thousands of messages ago and, when their diary holds that memory, they recall the *exact words* that were said — not a paraphrase — along with how it made them feel. The diary is the feelings; the receipts behind each memory now unlock the verbatim lines when the conversation truly reaches for them (it's deliberately selective — one moment at a time, only when you're clearly asking). Needs the RAG memory model installed for the "reaching for it" detection; and the same retrieval got smarter about never quoting the same lines twice in one prompt.
- ☕ **Away-time scenes now live inside your story.** While you're AFK, the character's solitary moments stop defaulting to meals-and-naps: night scenes wind down toward sleep (and sleeping through a night means a dream waits for you), a storm keeps them indoors with rain on the windows, a fine day pulls them outside, an active fixation intrudes on idle thoughts, and quiet moments let a recent memory surface and color their mood — so consecutive scenes vary instead of repeating. You can also choose **how much story time each away scene covers**: a few hours (as before), half the day, or a full day — a "full day" pace turns three away scenes into a three-day montage, with fresh weather and dreams along the way. Set it in the sidebar's AFK panel or the web app's settings. Deterministic as always — the model narrates the time, it never decides it.
- 🧭 **Ambitions are visible from the first message.** A character's ambitions now show right in the sidebar's Character State card (and on each group member's card) — 🧭 the ambition, its stage in words, and a slim progress bar — from the moment you open a fresh chat, so you always know the drive is armed. Same rows in the web app's chat tools. Previously nothing showed until the first real progress landed.
- 📔 **The Journal no longer remembers things that never happened.** If you regenerated, swiped, edited, or deleted a message after the character's diary had already read it, the diary kept its memory of the discarded version — a "memory" of an event that was rewritten out of your story. Now any rewrite cleans up after itself: memories citing the changed lines are removed (even pinned ones — a pin can't keep a phantom true), the diary re-reads the new timeline on its next pass, and manually planted memories, dreams, and ambition progress are never touched. Works in 1:1 and group chats alike.
- 🧭 **Ambitions — your character finally wants something bigger than today.** Give any character long-term ambitions in the character editor ("open my own bakery", "win back her trust") and they stop living scene to scene: the ambition quietly steers their choices, their off-screen AFK hours can go toward it, and it even colors their dreams. Progress moves the honest way — when the objectives they pursue actually complete — through stages you'll feel in their voice (just beginning → gaining ground → halfway there → nearly there), with waypoint moments landing in the Journal and "Our Story" timeline. And when they finally arrive? It becomes part of who they are — a permanent Growth Ring. Works per-character in group chats.
- 🏆 **"Our Story" — your whole journey on one timeline.** The Journal now has a second tab: every moment that mattered, in order — 🌱 growth, 📔 strong memories, 🌙 dreams, ⚡ Chance Time strikes, 🎯 achieved objectives — grouped by story day. Tap any entry with a receipt and the chat jumps straight to the line where it happened. Per-character in group chats (pick whose story from the Journal's owner dropdown), and on the web app under the new "Our story" section in chat tools. Nothing new is tracked — it's a fresh view over what your story already saved.
- 🌙 **Your character dreams now.** When a story night passes, they wake with a dream — a short, hazy scene that appears in the chat before their morning reply, woven from what actually mattered: their strongest Journal memories, their fixation, the mood they carried to sleep, even last night's weather. The dream also lands in their diary, so it can resurface later like any memory. Dreams never invent new facts — they only remix what your story already holds. In group chats, whoever ended the previous day is the one who dreams. On by default with the Journal and Passage of Time; toggle under Settings → General → Dreams.
- 🕰️ **"It's been a few days — where we left off:"** Coming back to a chat after time away, a small welcome-back banner now reminds you where the story stands (it shows the Journal's recap), so you're never lost re-entering a long roleplay. And if you opt in (off by default), the character themself will briefly, warmly acknowledge you've been gone — "it's been a while" — once, and then let it go: never asking where you were, never guessing what you were doing. **Privacy, plainly:** both features only look at the date on your last message — which your chat has always saved on your own device. Nothing new is collected, nothing is sent anywhere, and times only ever show as gentle words ("a few days"), never counts. Settings → General: "Welcome-back recap" and "Character notices your absence", plus how long counts as away.
- 🌦️ **The story has weather now.** With Passage of Time on, every story day gets its own weather — fronts roll through over a few days (a storm lingers, then breaks), seasons follow the story calendar, and your character *feels* it: the scene prompt knows it's a cold steady rain, comfort wears down faster in rough weather, and a clear day keeps their spirits up longer. See today's sky at a glance next to the scene clock in the sidebar — on desktop and the web app. Weather is woven from your chat's own story (no internet, no forecast service — deterministic, so reloading the chat never changes the sky). On by default with Passage of Time; turn it off any time in Settings → General → Story Weather.
- 🎨 **Create a character AND their whole face in one sitting — the new "Portrait & Avatars" step.** Both creators (the manual wizard and the AI Character Creator) now end with one shared panel: upload your own portrait or generate one, and — completely independently — generate a full expression pack (the same 8-base or all-28 presets as Image Studio) right there, before you've ever opened a chat. Your character is **saved first**, so a failed generation can never cost your writing; cancel any time and whatever finished stays in the gallery. Uploading works too: tag extra images as gallery looks or expressions, or drop in an emotion-named ZIP.
- 📖 **Smarter lorebooks from the AI Character Creator.** Generated world lore used to be a flat pile of paragraphs that only showed up if you typed the exact keyword — and vanished otherwise. Now the creator builds an always-present **world premise** entry (so the setting's tone is always in play, even when nothing specific is happening) and ranks the rest by importance, so core lore — factions, places — never gets squeezed out of the context before minor flavor does. Entries also fire **in context** now (a specific NPC surfaces only when they'd actually come up, not on every stray keyword), interchangeable **rumors/ambient flavor rotate** — only one at a time instead of dumping all of them at once — and the lore is **interconnected**: bringing up a faction can pull in the ritual it guards, which pulls in the relic behind that, so the world reads as a connected place rather than a list of isolated facts.
- 🎲 **Optional "living detail" macros in generated cards.** New opt-in toggle in the AI Character Creator (**off by default**): when on, the AI can sprinkle `{{pick:…}}` / `{{roll:…}}` macros into world lore and the opening message, so small details vary each playthrough — a tavern's daily special, a passing sound, a random count. Off by default because capable models use them well while smaller models may place them awkwardly; the toggle's tooltip explains the trade-off. Works on desktop and the web creator.
- 🖐️ **You get a say on the portrait before the expression pack.** Generating a portrait + pack together used to blow straight past the base image into the whole expression set. Now it **pauses on the portrait** and asks: like it? Tweak the prompt and **Regenerate**, use it to **build the expressions**, or just keep the portrait and skip the pack. No more waiting out eight faces built on a base you didn't want.
- ⚡ **Character creation is fast again on oMLX / LM Studio.** With a "thinking" model (Qwen3, etc.) on a local MLX/vLLM server, the AI Character Creator was secretly letting the model *think* through every step — slow, and it sometimes dumped raw reasoning into the portrait prompt. Front Porch now switches thinking off the way those servers actually understand, so creation is quicker and the portrait prompt stays clean.
- 🧠 **Image Studio finally remembers a Create model AND an Edit model separately.** Picking an edit model for the Edit tab used to silently break normal image generation (edit models can't paint from scratch) — and vice versa. Each tab now keeps its own model, your current setup migrates automatically, and a friendly warning appears if a model that looks like an edit/inpaint model ends up in the Create slot.
- 🎭 **Expression packs on remote APIs** — with an edit-capable model (like qwen-image-max-edit) selected as your Edit model, the pack dialog now works on remote image providers too, instead of refusing outright.
- 🖼️ **Draw Things connects in the installed app now** — the new built-in connection had two stacked bugs (a certificate check that could never pass, and Draw Things' compressed replies being rejected), so installed builds couldn't reach Draw Things at all. Both fixed and verified against a live Draw Things server; connection tests and generations show up in the Engine Status panel.
- ⭐ **Starring an avatar now really changes the card — everywhere** — the ★ in the Avatar Gallery updates the character's face across the whole app: library card, folder previews, open chats (sidebar, header, message bubbles, character pickers), the web app, and exports. One face per character. You can also finally delete the original portrait: the starred avatar steps up as the new portrait in its place. Bonus: star clicks no longer repaint the home screen behind the gallery.
- 🚩 **The "NSFW Tasks" switch stays on** — the objectives panel's NSFW toggle (and the task count) quietly reset after every message; they now hold for your whole session. (They still start OFF on each app launch, on purpose.)
- 📅 **A real calendar and clock** — your story now has actual dates: "9:40 PM, Tuesday, March 3rd" instead of just "night, Day 5". Tap the date in the sidebar to open the Story Calendar: a month view of your whole story, with a dot on every day your character wrote a journal memory — tap a day to read what they remember from it. Time also passes more naturally now: a long dinner conversation moves the clock an hour, a quick exchange barely nudges it, and "we drive for several hours" or "a week later" skips exactly that far. Set the story's start date to any year — 1887 works as well as today — from the calendar or when creating a character, and characters remember not just how something felt, but *when* it happened ("Day 5, last Tuesday night — 9 days ago"). Works on desktop and in the web app.
- 🐛 **Fixed: global background leaking into un-themed chats** — if you had ever set chat background in the global Settings dialog, every conversation that didn't have its own per-chat theme would show that background, even when it didn't make sense. Unthemed chats now correctly show no background, and only chats with an explicit theme override display their scene.

- 🎨 **Per-chat visual themes** — every conversation can now have its own look. Pick from 10 built-in presets (Fantasy, Galactic, Neon Grid, Sakura, Noir, Enchanted Forest, Ocean Depths, Cyberpunk, Roman Empire, Steampunk) or customize every detail: bubble colors, text colors, font, background scene, and decorative border style. Your settings carry over when you start a new chat with the same character or group, and each chat remembers its own theme independently.
- 🌐 **Themes on the web app too** — pick a preset and customize colors, font, and the background scene right from the chat tools button in the web app, so each conversation gets its own look in the browser too. (Decorative bubble borders are approximated in the browser; the desktop app draws the full painted versions.)
- 🎯 **Regenerating a reply now rolls back its quest changes** — if a character proposed a new objective or checked off a quest step during a reply you then regenerated, those changes used to stick around even though the moment they came from was gone. Now they're undone along with the rejected reply, and the new reply earns its own — your hand-made objectives and manual "Check now" results are never touched.
- 🎨 **Draw Things connection fixed for real** — connecting to Draw Things was a coin flip ("Test Connection" randomly failing) and image generation would show Draw Things rendering away while Front Porch never received the picture, then refuse to reconnect. One tiny byte-counting bug in how the app read Draw Things' replies caused all of it. Connection tests, model listing, single images, and expression packs now come through reliably.
- 🧠 **Memory (RAG) setup fixed on Windows** — the new built-in memory engine failed its self-test on every Windows machine ("Setup Failed" in the memory setup dialog) because of a Windows-only quirk in how the AI runtime is handed the model's file path. The engine now loads correctly on Windows — and the same fix also repairs character expressions and photo captions there, which were quietly affected by the same bug. If setup does fail, the dialog now shows the actual reason instead of a generic message.
- 🔧 Under-the-hood fixes and polish.
- 📚 **Same-name character imports no longer eat each other.** Importing several cards that share a name (or re-importing a variant next to one you already have) used to quietly keep only one — the progress bar said success, the library showed a single card. Now each file becomes its own library entry unless it's the *same* Front Porch character (matched by the card's private id, so editing realism and re-importing still keeps your chats). On a **single-file** import when the name already exists, you'll get a clear choice: **Keep both**, **Replace existing** (chats stay), or **Cancel**. Multi-file / folder import always keeps every card. Same behavior on the web library.
- 🤝 **Promises stick.** When someone really gives their word — not casual "maybe later," but a clear commitment — the character keeps it in their diary. Open promises quietly color the next replies (trust, tension, willingness). If that word is kept or broken, trust and bond move for real, and the moment lands in **Our Story** so it doesn't vanish. Chips still show the per-message deltas; this is the long scar, not a quest log. Works in groups for whoever is speaking. Needs Realism + Journal on; most ordinary turns cost nothing.
- 💞 **Big relationship shifts now show up in "Our Story".** When bond, long-term bond, or trust truly crosses a named tier — not every tiny chip under a message, only the real steps like *Warm → Friendly*, a deeper long-bond settling, or *Neutral → Guarded* — that moment is written into the diary timeline so you can find it weeks later. Chips still show the per-message deltas; Our Story keeps the chapter marks. Same in group chats (for whoever was speaking). Regenerating a reply won't invent reverse milestones. Desktop and web timelines both pick them up automatically.
- 🧠 **Long-term memory (RAG) stops dragging the story backward.** In long chats, retrieved memories could make a character repeat themselves or revive events the story had moved past — because the "memories per turn" setting silently did nothing (a fixed 8 was always used), barely-related old scenes passed the relevance filter, and the lines *just* scrolled out of view were the likeliest thing to be quoted back. All fixed: the setting works now (desktop, group settings, and web), only genuinely relevant and genuinely *older* moments come back, and memories can never crowd out the live conversation. Group chats get the same treatment, and their memory settings panel now actually applies what you set (parts of it were disconnected).
- 🍎 **Closing the app on macOS no longer counts as a crash.** Quitting with the red close button (or Cmd+Q) made macOS log a crash report every single time — a shutdown quirk in the AI engines' native cleanup, not a real crash, and nothing was ever lost. The app now finishes its normal save-everything shutdown and steps around the faulty native teardown, so quit means quit.
- 🔧 **Tool calling no longer gets stuck on "Not supported" after creating a visiting character.** Creating a Scene Guest mid-chat could knock the tool-calling status to "Not supported" and leave it there — the character creation was quietly interrupting a background request, and the app mistook that interruption for "this model can't do tool calling." Now character creation waits its turn instead of interrupting, and a dropped connection, a busy server, or a timeout is never counted against the model — the app simply retries tool calling on the next pass.
