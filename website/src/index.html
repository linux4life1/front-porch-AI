🏡 In the app and in your browser

The Stoop — the neighbours brought characters

A community character hub built right into the app — and now open from any browser at hub.frontporchai.app. Browse featured cards, follow creators you love, vote and share, and download straight into your library. Live counters update in real time as the neighbourhood votes. Whole group casts travel too: members, lorebooks, and their living Realism state arrive intact, ready to play. Only the hub touches the network; everything else stays on your machine.

Why people stay

Everything you need, nothing watching you

One app, out of the box. No extensions to hunt down, no servers to configure by hand.

🔒

Private by default

AI models run locally on your GPU. Offline use collects nothing and sends nothing. Remote APIs exist — but only if you turn them on.

🧠

Realism Engine

Characters build bond and trust, hold grudges, carry moods between scenes, get hungry and tired, and grow new traits as your story unfolds.

📚

Memory that lasts

Local long-term memory means characters recall the promise you made three sessions ago — without a cloud service reading along.

🎭

Living expressions

Portraits swap in real time with the character's emotions. Works with standard SillyTavern expression packs.

🗣️

Voice, both ways

Natural offline text-to-speech in dozens of voices, push-to-talk dictation, and a hands-free voice call mode.

👥

Group chats

Put a whole cast in one room. They talk to you and each other — each with their own moods, needs, and voice.

✍️

Porch Stories

Turn your chats into an illustrated novel with a page-flip reader and audiobook narration.

🖼️

Image generation

Connect A1111, Forge, SDNext, or Draw Things and get scene art and avatars without leaving the chat.

📱

Phone & browser

The built-in web app brings your library to any browser on your network — pick up the same chat from the couch.
