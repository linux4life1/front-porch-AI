# Rawhide — What's New (Nightlies)

These notes feed the in-app "Update Available" dialog for Rawhide / cutting-edge builds.
**Only list what landed after the last shipped nightly.** Clear this section when a new nightly goes out — delete the old bullets; do not accumulate them.

Last shipped nightly: `rawhide.20261006.fbe655b`. Everything below is unreleased.

## Recent improvements (unreleased — ships in the next build)

- 🎨 **Image Studio opens from Home** — the sidebar's **Image Studio** button (it replaces Image batches) opens the same Image Studio a chat opens, with no chat needed. Tap **Character…** to pick who to draw from your library. Batches are now the studio's fourth tab, **Batch**, next to Create, Edit and Expressions, so they share its backend, model, LoRAs and downloads, and the Batch tab shows the backend and model it will use right at the top. The chat's Image Studio has the Batch tab too. On the web, **Image Studio** replaces Images in the menu and holds the image desk that used to sit on the Models page, with the same Batch tab. On the desktop and the phone.
- **Global prompt additions** — Settings → General can append system and post-history instructions across characters and groups without editing their cards. Leave either field blank to disable its addition. Available on desktop and web/mobile.
- 🟤 **Dark mode is porch brown now, not blue-slate** — the dark grounds (pages, cards, dialogs, inputs, the chat header and sidebar) use the same warm studio browns as the AI Character Creator instead of the old blue slate. Message bubbles are unchanged: your bubble is still blue and the reply bubble is still slate. Saved bubble colors, named chat themes and light mode paper all stay as they were. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🟤 **The chat's last cool corners go warm** — the header, the text field and the sidebar body already turned warm brown with the grounds bullet above; this finishes what was left: the no-microphone dialog is now the warm card and reads in light mode too, the author's-note field's focus ring is porch amber (its Subtle slider stays blue), and the chat menu's Context Budget and Add Character icons are amber instead of cyan and purple. The director's Next button stays purple so it never looks like Play. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🏆 **Characters feel their goals land** — when a goal the character chose for themselves comes off, they feel proud of it (strongly, for their main goal), with the goal as the reason; when they have to let one go, they are a little disappointed. A stronger feeling already there stays: someone furious with you who also finishes something is still furious. With the Journal on, each one leaves a diary card in their own words ("I pulled it off: …"). The Objectives card shows how it has gone so far in this chat, like **3 landed · 1 let go**, and when the last three all went the same way the model is told so in words, never as a score. Goals you set are story direction and do not count. Regenerate or delete the reply and the feeling, the card and the count go back with the goal. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🎯 **Objectives keep up with the story** — every few messages, and right after a time skip, the app now looks at all of a character's goals at once and asks where each one stands. Steps done in any order are ticked, a next step the story has overtaken is rewritten (only for goals the character chose; your own steps are never reworded), a goal that is won, or no longer makes sense, is closed as won or let go, and a goal of the character's own that the story has wandered away from is **set aside**: it stays on the list, dimmed, and the model stops hearing about it until the story comes back to it. After "two weeks later" the goals are looked at again with that much time in mind. A character no longer files the goal it already has under new words, or a pile of side quests, and it is told what it finished or let go lately. Your goals reach the model as story direction and the character's own as what they want, in their own words. The **Retire a leftover quest after** setting is gone, because it is no longer needed. Regenerate undoes all of it, as before. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🎛️ **Generation settings show only what your backend uses** — Settings → Generation and each chat's own settings now hide the sliders your current backend never receives, instead of letting you move them for nothing. KoboldCpp shows everything. oMLX shows everything except Dynamic Temperature and Banned Phrases, and XTC now reaches oMLX too. Remote APIs show Min-P, Top-K and Repeat Penalty (not every provider uses them). xAI shows only Temperature, Top-P, Max Output Tokens and Context Size; api.openai.com adds Repeat Penalty. A hidden setting keeps its value: switch back and it is there as you left it. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🔄 **Image Studio sees new models without leaving** — add a model or LoRA to ComfyUI (or Draw Things or Automatic1111) while Image Studio is open, and Change model and the LoRA list now look again each time they open, with a **Refresh models** or **Refresh LoRAs** button on each for a file that lands while the list is on screen. A list that cannot be read, or comes back empty, keeps the files already shown. Before, the new file stayed hidden until you pressed Check or left Image Studio and came back, which threw away your prompt. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🪟 **Rawhide names itself in the title bar** — the window title now reads **Front Porch AI Rawhide**, so with stable and Rawhide both running you can tell them apart in the title bar, in the taskbar and when switching between apps, including inside a chat. Stable still says Front Porch AI. On the desktop.
- 🧭 **Local models answer what you just said** — your newest message now comes last in what the model reads, right before the character replies, instead of above the story notes (recap, journal, character state, author's note). Smaller local models on KoboldCpp or oMLX were answering those notes and their own long replies instead, so they would replay something that already happened (sending a text twice, reading the same messages again) or slip back in time. In a group, your message and the replies since it move together. Regenerate and Continue follow the same order. The cost on a local backend is re-reading about one extra message per reply, not the whole chat.
- 🔁 **Repetition settings now work on oMLX** — Repeat Penalty Tokens and DRY Strength never reached oMLX before. Its repeat penalty looked back only about 20 tokens, so a reply could copy a whole earlier paragraph again and again. Repeat Penalty Tokens now sets how far oMLX looks back, and DRY Strength turns on oMLX's repeat block, which stops a reply copying a long run of words that is already in the chat (at 0.8, about fifteen words; a stronger setting allows fewer). oMLX's multi-machine mode does not support the repeat block, so keep DRY Strength at 0 there.
- 🎚️ **The AI Character Creator's Realism switch starts the way Porch Life has it** — with the Realism Engine on in Settings → Porch Life, the creator's Realism step now opens with the engine on, so bond, trust, starting mood, Needs and the rest are shown there and written into the card; with it off, the step opens off. You can still flip the switch while you make the character, and Clear Everything goes back to your Porch Life setting. Before, the step always started off, so a character made here came without those starting values even when the engine is on for everything else. On the desktop (the phone's creator has no Realism step).
- 🔖 **Creator templates** — the AI Character Creator's Mode step now opens with **Start from a template**: every box on the form, in every step, the way you saved it (the mode, your vision and the interview questions, output settings, lore, 18+ and the rest; not the character's name). Pick one and the Configure step shows "Started from", with **Save as template** to write your changes back under a name, or a new name. Rename and delete from the tile's menu. Fifteen built-in archetype templates (Tsundere, Kuudere, Yandere, Femme Fatale, Dark Lord and the rest) are there from the start. A template saved on the phone shows on the desktop and the other way round; they live as files in your library's `chargen/templates` folder. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🎤 **The AI Character Creator asks your questions, in your character's voice** — the interview that runs before the profile is written now sits on the Configure step (desktop) and the Output step (phone) as an **Interview** card: eleven standard questions you can edit, drag into a new order, remove or add to, up to twelve, with **Reset to the standard questions** to go back. The questions and the answers follow the Narrative voice you picked: first or third person, present or past, with he, she or they from the Sex field (leave it blank for they/them). A question you edit is kept as written. Remove them all and the profile comes straight from your settings, with no interview. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🧹 **No more stray `think>` at the start of a reply** — Kimi K2.6 thinking on Nano-GPT, with thinking hidden, left the tail of its closing tag (`hink>`) at the head of chat replies and of every field the AI character creator wrote. The app now drops it as the reply arrives, and replies already saved with it display clean. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🎭 **Moods with a reason, that linger** — a character's mood now carries why they feel it ("furious, because he lied about the money") and holds from one reply to the next instead of being decided from scratch every turn. A feeling the story keeps feeding stays strong; one that is left alone eases over a few replies, and sleeping on it takes it down a notch. The model is handed the mood with its cause and, when it is moderate or strong, with the pull it has on how the character acts, in their own way: a furious character may refuse, argue or walk out, and nothing makes them cooperate. A month-long skip arrives neutral. The Character State card, the group member card and the phone show the cause and how long the feeling has held. Finishing or dropping the day's plan now moves the mood with that as the reason. On the desktop and the phone.
- **Read chat during evaluations** — Minimize evaluation keeps Realism or objective work visible in a compact bar while you review the conversation. Show evaluation details restores the expanded view; existing Stop and Skip controls remain available. The view is remembered across chats and restarts and shared with web/mobile. Long waits show elapsed time after 30 seconds.
- ⏩ **Big time skips read smoothly** — write "two weeks later" (or "a few days later", "3 months pass", "skip ahead a year") and the story clock now moves the whole span; before, any number of weeks counted as one, and days, months and years did not count at all without an OOC note. For a skip of a day or more, a short narrator card fills in how those days went before the character replies. Everyone arrives at their everyday hunger, energy and the rest instead of worn down as if they had skipped a meal, and the mood, fixation and spot they were left in from the last scene are let go. Regenerate keeps the same card and the same skip. Settings → Porch Life → **Time-skip bridge** turns the card off; the skip itself still lands. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🎭 **Turn a character into a persona, and a persona into a character** — a library card's menu has **Use as Persona**: it saves a persona with the character's name, description and personality, birthday and portrait, and offers to swap {{char}} and {{user}} so the text still points at the right people. The character itself is not changed. Going the other way, a persona's menu on the Personas page has **Make a Character**: **Manual** opens the character wizard with the name, description, birthday and picture filled in, and **AI Assist** hands the persona to the AI character creator, which writes the personality, scenario and greetings around it. On the desktop and the phone (the phone's persona list is in Settings).
- 📊 **See the prompt behind any reply** — each reply now has an Inspect prompt button that opens the Context Budget for the exact prompt that produced it, not only the latest one. Every reply keeps its token counts; the 20 newest also keep the full text of each section, so you can read and copy what the model was sent. A regenerated take has its own; Continue keeps the original. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🔀 **See the words a reply nearly used (KoboldCpp)** — with a local KoboldCpp model, a reply now remembers where the model was unsure. The **Other words** button on the reply lists those places with the words it weighed and how likely each was. On the last reply, tap one to rewrite the reply from that word: everything before it stays, the model carries on from your pick, and the new take is added as a swipe, just like Regenerate. Other backends do not send this, so the button only appears on KoboldCpp replies. On the desktop and the phone.
- 📊 **Your chats together** — a character's Details tab now shows what the two of you have said: how many chats, messages and words each of you wrote, the longest chat, and when you first and last talked. It counts your 1:1 chats (group chats are not counted), only the reply you kept on each message, and not the thinking. A character you have never talked to shows nothing new. On the desktop and in the phone's character editor.
- 🎲 **Chaos Mode, reworked** — the wheel has over 240 everyday events now, up from about 150, in new kinds: social mix-ups, weather and surroundings, plot hooks, and the body's needs (sudden hunger, a broken toilet, a jammed bathroom lock, a crashing headache). Some events happen to you or, in a group, to two characters at once. Each wheel mixes eight different kinds, and an event you just had won't come straight back. Settings → Porch Life has a **How often** choice under Chaos Mode (Rare, Normal or Wild), and after an event the story gets a couple of messages' breather before pressure builds again. The model is now told to fit an event to the story's setting instead of dropping a phone into a fantasy tavern. Spicy events (now over 80) appear only with **18+ themes** on, and the spicy switch is hidden without it; a 1:1 chat now remembers its spicy switch. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🎡 **Chance Time respects the refractory** — right after a climax, the spicy wheel no longer lands on the four events that claim the character is aroused or "suddenly in the mood": their desire is honestly at zero during the cooldown, and prose that contradicted the Lust bar made the bar stop feeling real. Every other event — all 78 remaining adult ones and every normal one — still spins as usual, because comedy from an afterglow works; contradiction from it doesn't. Multiorgasmic characters keep the full wheel, since their desire genuinely stays up. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🧹 **A tidier Advanced tab** — Settings → Advanced no longer repeats the context, GPU layers, KV cache and graphics-card readout that the Local model card, the preset editor and the model settings window already have. Acceleration is always picked for your card now (CUDA on NVIDIA, Metal on a Mac, Vulkan on AMD and Intel); the old backend picker is gone, and a choice it saved is dropped. AMD cards keep one switch, **Use ROCm instead of Vulkan**, in Advanced Launch Options. The Min Output Tokens slider is gone too: no backend ever received it.
- 🧑‍💻 **Waifu Coder turns always finish** — a turn no longer spins forever if OpenCode stops mid-task or the wrap-up goes quiet; it ends and says so, and the next message starts OpenCode again. Speech with emoji, kaomoji or Japanese no longer loses words. Stop no longer gets a late wrap-up afterwards. Undo now takes back the whole turn, including the files it changed, instead of only the closing words. Each tool shows once in the log instead of once per step. And your coworker remembers the conversation after you restart the app.
- 🧑‍💻 **Waifu Coder switches do what they say** — switching between This folder only and Whole disk, or Library tools now applies on your next message, and your coworker keeps the conversation. Plan mode really is read-only: it can look around but cannot edit files or run commands. Yolo and Build switch instantly, even mid-turn. The safety text now lists exactly which commands are refused, and says plainly that nothing guards secret files. Forget project now really forgets, and Chat Settings are saved.
- 🧑‍💻 **Waifu Coder installs a tested OpenCode** — Porch now downloads OpenCode 1.18.30, the version Waifu Coder is built against, instead of whatever is newest, and checks the download's fingerprint before using it. If you already have a newer OpenCode, Settings offers to switch to 1.18.30.
- 🧑‍💻 **Waifu Coder stays in character** — Stop, undo, errors and lost connections are now told as short narration around your coworker ("Mira sets the keyboard down.") instead of lines in their mouth, with the technical message a tap away. A line above the message box says what they are doing right now, like "Mira is running tests…", even with the sidebar closed. Permission asks show their portrait and the whole command, Allow once is the default, and Y or N answers from the keyboard. The closing line after each turn now knows which files changed and what failed, and matches how they spoke earlier in the session; if it never arrives, the turn still ends with a line. Editing the character card, or leaving a session open overnight, reaches your coworker on the next message.
- 🧑‍💻 **Waifu Coder is easier to use** — the new-project steps have a Back button on every step, picking a folder is one tap, and the safety notes are three short points. Settings use plain words: **This folder only** or **Whole disk**, and **Start** instead of Sit down. Hover Plan, Build or Yolo to see what each lets your coworker do. Starting, reopening a project, turning on a language and downloading OpenCode all show that they are working, and say why if they fail. A failed tool shows a cross, not just a red dot; screen readers hear "In progress" instead of `in_progress`; Enter no longer sends while you are still typing with a Japanese or Chinese keyboard; the animations stop when your system asks for less motion; and small text is larger.
- 🧑‍💻 **Waifu Coder keeps your sessions safe** — sessions are saved whole, so the app closing mid-save can no longer leave a half-written file. If a saved session cannot be read, reopening it now says so instead of opening a blank session over it, and if a save fails you see why. Two characters with the same name no longer get mixed up: a session follows the card it was started with, even after a rename.

- 🖥️ **Sharper text while a model is running, on Windows and Linux too** — the app now draws with the same renderer it used before Flutter 3.47 on every desktop. The newer renderer smeared and ghosted text whenever the graphics card was busy with a local model; the Mac build switched last week, and Windows and Linux follow.

- ⚡ **Find the fastest settings for your computer** — the Local model card (in Settings, and on the phone's Models page) has a new button that tries a few ways of running your model on your graphics card, one at a time, and keeps the fastest. It tells you how long it will take and asks first (a few minutes; chat waits while it runs, and Cancel puts everything back). When it is done it says how much sooner replies come, and the result is saved as a preset for that model, so it is used every time that model starts. NVIDIA cards also start a little faster now, before any test. Experts can time batch sizes for their own presets in the preset editor.

- ⌨️ **Typing in the AI Character Creator no longer lags on Windows** — every key used to save the whole creator form, which on Windows rewrote the settings file dozens of times per letter. Typing now saves once you pause, and only what changed.
- 🪟 **A file window that fails now says so** — when the window for choosing a file or folder, or for saving, doesn't appear, you get a plain explanation with a Try again button instead of a click that does nothing. A folder the app can't read says so too. And exporting a character, place, chat or story whose name has characters Windows doesn't allow in a file name (such as `:` or `?`) now opens the save window instead of waiting forever.
- **The AI creator has a Greetings step**, on the desktop and the phone. After a character is generated, each greeting gets its own card: edit it, rewrite it (steer the rewrite with one line, like "start at the harbor at dawn"), delete an alternate, or add another one, written straight away. On the desktop, rewriting the first message leaves the outfit as it was, and the Realism step offers to read it again from the new opening.
- 🗂️ **Folders follow your sort** — the folders at the top of the library now sort the same way as the cards. Name puts them A→Z; Recent Activity, Import Date and Messages Sent go by what is inside each folder, subfolders included (its newest chat, its newest card, or its total messages), with empty folders last. Group chats sort A→Z. In the desktop app for now.
- 🔎 **Search where you mean to** — the button at the start of the library search box now works at the top level too: pick **Everywhere** or **Top level only** (just the cards that are in no folder, which is where imported cards land). Inside a folder, **This Folder Only** no longer finds cards in its subfolders; **Folder & Subfolders** does that. The app remembers your choice, like the sort. In the desktop app for now.
- ☑️ **Select all** — Multi-select and Organize into folders now have **Select all** and **Select none** at the top. Select all picks every card on screen, so after a search it picks only what the search found. Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on a Mac) does the same once you have clicked in the grid, and Esc clears the selection. Cards you picked stay picked when a new search or folder hides them, and the count says so, for example "12 selected (3 hidden)"; Move and Delete still include them, and their warnings give the full number. In the desktop app for now.
- 🚚 **Moving many cards is quick** — moving a big selection into a folder (or back out) now happens in one go instead of card by card, so the library no longer redraws once for every card. In the desktop app, and when you move cards from the phone or a browser.
- 🖱️ **Pick cards with the mouse** — in the library, a quick drag over the grid draws a box and picks every character and group chat it touches; hold Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) to add to what is already picked. Shift-click picks every card from the last one you clicked, and Ctrl/Cmd-click picks or unpicks one card without opening it. The box scrolls the grid near the top and bottom edges. In the desktop app.
- 📦 **Drag your picks into a folder** — press and hold a picked card, then drag: every pick comes along, shown as a small stack with the count, and the picked cards fade while you drag. Drop them on a folder, which says "Drop to move 3 here", or on any level of the folder path at the top ("My Characters" or a folder above the one you are in), and they all move in one go. Holding a card you have not picked still moves just that card, and Esc calls a drag off. In the desktop app.
- 💾 **KoboldCpp keeps less memory for chats you have left** — the chat you have open is always kept ready in memory, so a reply after a Realism check starts at once (nothing decides whether it is worth it any more), and that memory is freed when you leave the chat (another chat, another character, or back to the library). If you switch between a few chats, **Keep recent chats ready** keeps up to four of the ones you used last ready too, so going back to one is quick: Settings → Advanced → Advanced Launch Options on the desktop, or Settings on the phone.
- 🖋️ **Text on the Mac no longer smears while a local model is working** — the app draws with Flutter's older renderer on macOS. The newer one smeared or ghosted text whenever the graphics chip was busy or memory was tight, which on a Mac is every moment a local model is reading a prompt.
- 🧵 **New KoboldCpp presets ask for the right number of threads** — on Apple Silicon the preset editor now suggests the fast cores minus two for the app (the efficiency cores only slow the maths down), and on other machines it leaves one core for the app. Before, a preset could take every core but one, and the window froze while a long chat was being read. Presets you already made keep their number; open them in the editor to take the new suggestion.
- 🏷️ **The tag window scrolls** — a card imported with dozens of tags used to push the tag window off the bottom of the screen, with no way to save. The tags now scroll inside the window and the box and buttons stay put.

- 🪟 **No more white window at start on Windows** — sometimes the app opened as a blank white window until you maximized it or resized it. The window now appears once it has drawn, at its saved size and place.

- 🍽️ **Needs keep time with the story** — hunger, bladder and energy now wear down at a steady rate for every hour of story time (each character's Pace still sets how fast), instead of the AI guessing a number for each stretch. A reply in the same moment, or a Continue, wears nothing, and a skip off-screen never empties a bar. Sleep is something the story shows: if a reply says the character slept, their energy comes back; stay up all night and they will be worn out in the morning. Characters also bring a need up later and more gently: nothing is said above 55, so a need at 50 is a passing thought, not a demand. The chip under a reply shows the time part and the scene part apart (`1 hr 20 min · lunch`), and the sidebar bars turn amber at 40 and red at 25, on the desktop and the phone (where a nearly empty need no longer draws a full bar).
- ⏱️ **The refractory counts story minutes** — with 18+ themes and Afterglow on, the Refractory countdown after a climax now runs on the story clock (about 45 minutes to an hour and three quarters) instead of a number of replies, so a skip to the next morning ends it. With Passage of Time off it counts replies, as before.
- 🌡️ **Afterglow recovery now tells people apart** — the cooldown after a character's climax is judged from **who they are**: the judge now reads the card's intimate preferences and personality (or description when the personality is blank) and sets the length from their traits, instead of the same ~6 turns for everyone. The range widened from 3–7 to 0–12 turns (about 15 minutes of story time each): an insatiable character may brush it off in a quarter-hour, a reserved one may take a couple of hours, and **no cooldown at all** is now an answer it can give — such a character simply stays sated instead of getting a forced lock. In a group each member is judged from their own card. On the desktop and the phone.
- 🕰️ **A word when the clock is stopped** — with Passage of Time off, a short line now sits under every Needs switch (the chat sidebar, the character editor, the group Needs tab and Settings → Porch Life, on the desktop and the phone): "With Passage of time off, hunger, bathroom and energy wear a little each reply; the rest move only when the story says so."

- 🎭 **An Expressions workspace in Image Studio** — expression packs now have their own tab beside Create and Edit, with their own description, source picture and target character. Switch tabs and come back: the draft is still there, and a stopped pack keeps its results until you re-roll it, continue it, import it or choose Reset pack. The tab shows the Create or Edit settings the pack will use, and the shared controls hold still while pictures are being made. The phone's Studio page gets the same workflow.
- ✍️ **Prompt rules for expression packs** — tell a pack what to put before and after every expression prompt, and words to find and replace (in order; case-sensitive if you like). Save them as your defaults or use them for one pack only, and read the effective prompt for each expression before you start. A pack keeps the rules it started with; change them on a stopped pack and re-roll to try the new wording. The same rules are offered in the avatar creator and on the phone, where a pack you started from the phone can have its rules edited too. A prompt you edited for a single expression keeps its own wording.
- 🧰 **The app checks your KoboldCpp when it starts** — a copy older than the app can run (1.112 and up) gets a box that says so and offers the update, done right there, or to remove KoboldCpp if you use another backend. That box has no Not now, because the engine would refuse to start anyway. A newer KoboldCpp that is simply out gets a gentler box with a Not now that holds for three days (a release after that one asks again). Nothing shows if KoboldCpp is not installed; it is optional.
- 🩹 **Needs stay put when the Realism Engine is off.** v1.5.0 let the story clock wear hunger, bladder and energy even with the engine off, so a reply could show a needs chip ("Bladder −1") in a chat that had Realism off; and a reply that only carried the time chip said "Bond unchanged" and "Trust unchanged" as if it had been scored. Both are gone: with the engine off nothing of Needs moves or shows, and the time chip is just the time. The Needs switch in Settings → Porch Life now takes effect on the next turn instead of the next time you open the chat. The same rule covers "Reprocess Needs" and its undo on an older reply's chip: while Needs are off (the engine, the chat's switch or the Porch Life switch) the chip offers neither, on the desktop and the phone, and both come back with Needs. The Needs bars in the sidebar hide too while Needs are off, rather than sitting frozen as if they were still running; they come back where they were when Needs do.
- ⏱️ **Needs with the clock off tick again.** v1.5.0 put hunger, bathroom and energy on the story clock, and with Passage of Time off nothing ticked between replies anymore: bars only moved when the story itself did something. That is back to how it worked before: with the clock off, every reply wears the speaker's hunger, bathroom and energy a little (hunger 2, bathroom 3, energy 3) and the judge scores what happened on top; regen does not charge it twice and a delete gives it back. With the clock on nothing changes. The line under the Needs switches says so, on the desktop and the phone.
- 🍎 **Mac: macOS 13.3 Ventura is now the floor** — the ONNX runtime the app ships (for memory search) is built for 13.3, so on Monterey the app could never load it. The floor now says what the app can actually run on; nothing changes on Ventura and newer.
- 🪪 **Read your character before the rest is written** — the AI Character Creator now stops after the profile on a new **Profile** step, so you see the Description, Personality and Scenario before any greeting, lore or picture prompt is made. Edit any box, or tell the rewrite what is wrong ("Does not follow the Tsundere personality type") and watch it write a new one; Stop keeps the old text. The step also shows everything else the interview filled in: birthday, job and hours, what they are doing as the story opens, how they stand with you, ambitions, what they warm to and what puts them off, 18+ preferences when 18+ is on, and a short pitch. Edit any of it, or read it again from the profile. Press Next and the rest is written from what you left. If you change the profile after the greetings exist, the Greetings step says so and offers to rewrite them all or keep them, and the example dialogue and lorebook can now be steered and rewritten too; a lorebook entry you edited by hand is kept. The whole creator also moves to the warm brown Porch Stories look. On the desktop and the phone, and a phone reload on the Profile step finds it again.
- 🛌 **You decide how a character comes down** — with 18+ themes and Afterglow on, the character editor and the AI creator's Realism step now carry a **Recovery after intimacy** block: a preset that picks how the cooldown works — Auto (the judge and the card's physiology decide), **None / multiorgasmic** (no lock at all), a lock whose length the personality judge sets, or a **fixed length** from 0 to 180 minutes on a slider.
