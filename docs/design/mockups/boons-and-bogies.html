Boons & Bogies

Characters get abilities that improve as the story earns them, and flaws that surface now and then on a roll. Both live on Growth Rings, the system that already tracks how a character changes within a chat.

Boon · advancement

Kaito's stealth

Climbs Novice → Skilled → Master as scenes reinforce it. Uses Growth's existing skill category and tier physics.

Bogey · flaw on a roll

Kaito is clumsy

A pressure roll decides when it shows up: never every turn, never twice in a row, but always eventually. A growing boon can wear it down until it's overcome.

The milestone moment

One screen on every surface, the way Chance Time works. The card, wording, buttons and their order are identical on desktop and web. The particles come from one shared, seeded simulation: Flame draws it on desktop and a canvas draws it on web, so the same moment bursts the same way everywhere. What you're looking at

  • Boon meters show ring strength; the tier marks fall on Growth's existing thresholds (0.35 and 0.80).
  • Bogey meters show roll pressure. A bogey on cooldown reads 0 and says so.
  • "kit" marks rings seeded from the character card. Kit bogies start pinned (📌), so they can't fade out early.
  • 2 of 3: code caps active bogies at three. The model can't add a fourth.
  • Overcome bogies are retired: they stay in history and stop reaching the prompt.

The kit on the character card

Card authors can pre-seed boons and bogies in the realism step of the creator and editor. The kit travels with the card through export, import and Stoop review. Each new chat copies it into starting rings, and each chat's character then grows differently from there.

Plan

Build phases

Each phase leaves the app working, desktop and web together.

0
Repoint the Growth comments

The old Growth Rings doc stays deleted. Its 19 references in the code move to this doc. Comments only.

1
Pull out the pressure roll

Shared helper, with golden vectors that fail if any Chance Time roll changes.

2
Bogey category and Growth panel

New category, roll state in metadata, the three-bogey cap, prompt and parser support, and the settings switches (default off).

3
The card kit

Card field, seeding at chat start, creator and editor on both surfaces, group card manifest.

4
Bogey rolls

Injection, regen and swipe re-inject the same bogey, Continue never rolls, heavy scenes hold the roll.

5
Milestone popup

Flame on desktop, a canvas twin on web, Reduce Motion, a queue, and Redo.

6
Boons wear bogies down

The linked boon slows bogey pressure; reaching Master can propose that the bogey is overcome.

Decided 2026-11-19

Rulings

ApprovalNew boons, tier-ups, new bogies and bogies overcome always ask first, even with review-first off. Other rings are unchanged.
Tier namesNovice, Skilled, Master. The tier maths underneath stays the same.
Roll numbersBase 3, growth 4, cooldown 4. The Chaos frequency setting doesn't change them.
DefaultOff in the first release.
Heavy scenesA bogey that would fire is held, and its pressure kept, until the scene calms.
Growth docStays deleted. This proposal becomes the Growth design reference.
